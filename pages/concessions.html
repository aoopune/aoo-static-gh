01

Discounts

Rate discounts

The rate depends on your situation.

  • Woman as primary applicant The rate is often 0.05% to 0.10% lower than normal. She is usually the sole owner or the first name on the property papers.
  • Salary with the same bank You may get a preferential rate.
  • Existing savings or loan customer Some banks add a loyalty cut at sanction for long-standing customers.
  • Green home The rate is often 0.05% to 0.10% lower than normal. The project must be rated by the Indian Green Building Council (IGBC), GRIHA, LEED, or another approved Indian agency.

The rate after discounts can differ from the listed rate.

02

Subsidy

PMAY interest subsidy PMAY (U)↗ (opens official PMAY page)

Under the Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme, eligible first-time buyers can get an interest subsidy credited to the loan account — not cash in hand.

Who it targets

  • EWS, LIG, or MIG income Household income and home price decide which slab you fit. Each category has its own caps.
  • First home, often with a woman owner Usually no pucca house in your name anywhere in India. Woman ownership or co-ownership is required in many cases.
  • Loan within the subsidy cap Subsidy is calculated on a capped amount by category — not always on your full sanction.

How it works

  • At loan sanction The bank checks eligibility and registers the subsidy claim with the nodal agency.
  • Subsidy hits the loan account The amount lowers your effective EMI or outstanding balance — not cash in hand.
  • Aadhaar and income proof ready The bank files the claim. You do not apply to PMAY separately for the subsidy.
03

Waivers

Fee waivers

Banks sometimes run limited-time offers on charges beyond interest.

  • During a festival or campaign Processing fee may be zero or discounted. GST may or may not be included in the waiver.
  • Salary with the same bank Processing fee may be waived when your salary is credited with that bank.
  • Offer with free legal or valuation Those fees may be waived. Others still charge them separately.
  • Moving your loan to another bank Takeover fees on the old loan plus fresh charges on the new one can cancel a small waiver.

For general understanding only. Concession rules depend on your income, property, and the lender’s current policy. Banks and government schemes change over time.
