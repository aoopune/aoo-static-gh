What is the complaint path?

Write to your lender first. Wait about 30 days. Then approach the RBI or NHB only if it stays unresolved.

  1. Talk to the lender Many issues get fixed with the bank or housing finance company.
  2. Put the same problem in writing Get it on record with a ticket or acknowledgment.
  3. Wait for a final reply About 30 days for a final reply.
  4. Approach the regulator Bank → Reserve Bank of India (RBI). Housing finance company → National Housing Bank (NHB).

Where do you start?

Talk to the loan team first. Get it on record. Write an email if it still does not move.

  • Talk first Call or visit the loan team. Ask them to check the problem and tell you what they will do next.
  • Ask for a ticket number Note the date and what they said.
  • If they fix it Ask for written confirmation. You may not need the next steps.
  • Then put it in writing No ticket, only vague promises, or the same problem again. Write the same facts down next.

What must a complaint include?

Your name, loan number, what happened, and what you want fixed.

  • Who you are Your full name, mobile, email, and address.
  • Your loan Loan account number and branch.
  • What happened The facts with dates. Any money involved.
  • What you want fixed Refund, correction, papers back, written confirmation, or compensation you can prove.

Send it by email, app, website form, or a letter they acknowledge.

  • Loan papers Sanction letter and Key Fact Statement.
  • Your complaint trail The written complaint and the acknowledgment or ticket.
  • Their reply, or silence The final reply, or proof that 30 days passed with no answer.
  • Evidence pack Statements, charge receipts, emails, SMS.
  • If papers are delayed after closure Keep the loan-closed letter and proof you paid in full. Property papers should come back within 30 days. If the lender is late, RBI allows ₹5,000 a day. If papers are lost, the lender replaces them at its cost.
  • If someone files for you You need an authorisation letter. An advocate can file with the Ombudsman only if that advocate is the person who was wronged.

When do you go to the regulator?

After the lender's process: the RBI for banks, or the NHB for housing finance.

  • When you may file with RBI After about 30 days with no final reply. Or sooner, if the bank already gave a final reply and the issue is still not fixed.
  • Deadline to file Within 90 days of the bank’s last reply, or of the day the 30-day wait ended if there was no reply. Use whichever date is later.
  • Before you file Incomplete, anonymous, or suggestion-only complaints are rejected. So is a request beyond what the Ombudsman can grant.
  • Online File through RBI CMS. Keep the complaint number.
  • Email or post Send the complaint to CRPC by email or by post.
  • Helpline Explains the process. Does not register the complaint.
  • Size of the dispute No upper limit on the home loan dispute amount.
  • Consequential loss Proven consequential loss capped at ₹30 lakh.
  • Time, expense, harassment Additional compensation capped at ₹3 lakh.
  • If you receive an Award Accept it in writing to the bank within 30 days, or it lapses.
  • Bank after an Award 30 days to comply, or to appeal where the scheme allows.
  • Your appeal Certain Awards or rejections may go to the RBI Appellate Authority within 30 days, usually through CMS or the appellate email. Not every closure is appealable. Read the decision letter carefully.
  • File with NHB File through NHB GRIDS, or use the complaint cell form on nhb.org.in.
  • Appeal at NHB You may appeal to NHB’s Chief Grievance Redressal Officer within 30 days of the status intimation, as NHB states.

What is outside this path?

Some issues need a different route than Bank → RBI or NHB.

  • The bank refused the loan This path will not force them to sanction it.
  • You only want a better rate That is not a service complaint by itself. Compare and negotiate with lenders.
  • Wrong credit report Start with the credit bureau and the bank that sent the data. Then RBI CMS if you still need help.
  • Loan-linked insurance feels mis-sold Policy side → insurer and IRDAI. Lender’s service around the sale → still write to the lender.
  • Fraud, bribery, or criminal harassment Go to the police for the crime. Use bank vigilance for staff bribery. You can still raise the loan service side with the lender.
  • The same matter is already in court or a tribunal An Ombudsman filing on the same matter usually closes. Pick one main track.
  • Consumer Commission, civil court, or Lok Adalat Separate legal tracks. National Consumer Helpline and CPGRAMS can run beside this path.
