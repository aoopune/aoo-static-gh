Bank or housing finance company?

Open your sanction letter and EMI statement. Use the legal name of the lender, not the brand people use in conversation.

Bank home loan

Escalate to the RBI Ombudsman at cms.rbi.org.in after the bank stage.

Housing finance company

Escalate to NHB GRIDS at grids.nhbonline.org.in. Housing finance companies are outside the RBI Ombudsman scheme.

Wrong credit report

Dispute with the credit bureau and the bank that sent the data. Then use RBI CMS against the bureau or the bank if needed.

What you can complain about

The Ombudsman route covers deficiency in service. In plain words: the lender did not follow the rules, the contract, or fair service it was required to give.

  • Wrong or unexplained charges
  • Foreclosure or closure fees that clash with RBI directions on floating-rate individual home loans
  • Delay in sanction or disbursement without clear reason
  • Interest reset or calculation errors tied to published rules or your sanction terms
  • Delay returning original property papers after full repayment
  • Delay in NOC, closure letter, or removal of registered charge
  • Recovery agent harassment
  • Wrong reporting of your loan to a credit bureau by the bank

What this path usually will not decide

The Ombudsman will not force a bank to approve a home loan it rejected. It will not decide a pure bargaining fight over the offered rate as if that alone were a service case.

The same grievance already pending in a court or tribunal is normally closed as non-maintainable. Incomplete, anonymous, or suggestion-only filings fail. You must complain to the lender in writing before RBI.

The path for a bank home loan

1. Written complaint to the bank

Use email, app, website form, or a letter with acknowledgment. Name the loan account, dates, what went wrong, and the exact fix you want. Keep proof.

2. Bank internal review

Branch, customer care, then nodal officer if needed. If the bank rejects or only partly fixes the issue, it must send the case to its Internal Ombudsman before the final reply.

3. Final bank reply within 30 days

The bank must give a final decision within 30 days of receiving your complaint. You may escalate after 30 days of silence, or earlier if the reply is unsatisfactory.

4. RBI Ombudsman

File at cms.rbi.org.in, or email crpc@rbi.org.in, or post to the Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre in Chandigarh. No fee.

5. Award or appeal

If an Award is passed, accept it in writing to the bank within 30 days or it lapses. Some decisions can be appealed inside RBI within 30 days.

How to write the bank complaint

One clear letter beats many angry messages. Put everything that matters in a single thread you can attach later.

  • Full name, mobile, email, address
  • Loan account number and branch
  • What happened, with dates
  • Money involved, if any
  • Exact relief sought — refund, correction, papers, written confirmation, compensation
  • Copies of sanction letter, statements, charge proofs, earlier emails or SMS

Save the ticket number, sent-mail copy, courier receipt, or screen capture of the form submission.

RBI filing window under the 2026 scheme

From 1 July 2026, complaints follow the Reserve Bank Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2026.

Wait for the bank

30 days from receipt of your written complaint, unless a special RBI timeline is longer.

File with RBI

Within 90 days of the bank’s last communication, or of the end of that 30-day wait. Use whichever date is later.

Do not delay

Older practice allowed about 1 year. The 2026 window is shorter. Act when the bank stage ends.

Where and how to file with RBI

Online

https://cms.rbi.org.in — prefer this. Keep the complaint number.

Email

crpc@rbi.org.in with the prescribed details and attachments.

Post

Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre, Reserve Bank of India, Central Vista, Sector 17, Chandigarh 160017.

Helpline

Toll-free 14448 can guide you. It does not register the complaint itself.

You may use an authorised representative. An advocate can file only if the advocate is the aggrieved customer.

What RBI can award

There is no upper limit on the size of the home loan dispute you can bring. Compensation for proven consequential loss is capped at ₹30 lakh. Extra compensation for time, expense, and harassment is capped at ₹3 lakh.

Asking for more than the Ombudsman can grant can get the case rejected. Ask for what you can show with papers.

After an Award

Send a letter accepting the Award in full and final settlement to the bank within 30 days of receiving it. If you miss that, the Award can lapse.

The bank then has 30 days to comply or to appeal where the scheme allows. You may also appeal certain Awards or rejections to the RBI Appellate Authority within 30 days, usually through CMS or aaos@rbi.org.in. Not every closure is appealable. Read the letter.

If your loan is with a housing finance company

1. Write to the HFC

Use its published grievance or nodal contacts. Keep acknowledgment.

2. Wait about 30 days

Or escalate earlier if the reply is unsatisfactory.

3. File with NHB

Use grids.nhbonline.org.in or the NHB complaint cell form published on nhb.org.in.

4. NHB appeal

If still unhappy, prefer an appeal to NHB’s Chief Grievance Redressal Officer within the time stated on the status letter, often 30 days.

Property papers after you close the loan

After full repayment, the lender must return original property documents and remove the registered charge within 30 days. Delay attributable to the lender attracts compensation of ₹5,000 for each day of delay under RBI directions.

If papers are lost or damaged, the lender must help obtain duplicates at its cost. An extra 30 days may apply before the daily compensation clock in those cases. Put the closure date and return request in writing.

Other forums around this path

These do not replace the bank-to-RBI sequence for bank service cases. They sit beside it when the problem needs a different door.

National Consumer Helpline

1915 or consumerhelpline.gov.in. Pre-litigation help.

CPGRAMS

pgportal.gov.in. Central public grievance portal. Sometimes used with public sector banks.

Consumer Commission

Deficiency in service and unfair trade practice cases, with compensation.

Civil court

Contract and larger disputes.

Lok Adalat

Compromise settlement when both sides want closure.

Police

Fraud, forged papers, or criminal recovery harassment.

Bank vigilance

Staff corruption or bribery allegations, not ordinary EMI math.

Insurance regulator

Mis-selling of loan-linked insurance can also go through IRDAI pathways.

Filing the same civil grievance in court before or while using the Ombudsman can block the Ombudsman case. Choose the main track with care.

Documents to keep ready

  • Loan account number and ID proof
  • Sanction letter and Key Fact Statement
  • Your written complaint to the lender and the acknowledgment
  • Lender’s final reply, or proof of 30 days of silence
  • Statements, charge receipts, emails, SMS
  • Foreclosure letter and payment proof if papers are delayed
  • Clear calculation of loss and compensation claimed
  • Authorisation letter if someone files for you

Before you escalate to RBI

  • You already wrote to the bank and can prove it.
  • Thirty days have passed, or you have an unsatisfactory reply.
  • You are still inside the 90-day filing window.
  • The grievance is a service issue, not a pure loan refusal.
  • The same matter is not already pending in a court or tribunal.
  • Your attachments are complete and your relief ask is specific.
