Grievance redressal process

The stages from first complaint to the regulator.

Lodging a complaint

Many issues get fixed with the bank or HFC.

Complaint in writing

Put the same problem on record.

Final reply

About 30 days for a final reply.

Approach the regulator

Bank → Reserve Bank of India (RBI). Housing finance company → National Housing Bank (NHB).

Lodging a complaint

Register the issue with your bank or HFC first.

Talk first

Call or visit the loan team. Ask them to check the problem and tell you what they will do next.

Ask for a ticket number

Note the date and what they said.

If they fix it

Ask for written confirmation. You may not need the next steps.

Then put it in writing

If there is no ticket, only vague promises, or the same problem again — write the same facts down next.

Escalation matrix

Internal levels at the bank or HFC. Contacts are on their website.

Bank

Branch or customer care

Where your written complaint usually starts.

Nodal officer

Next if nothing moves. Listed by zone, circle, or network.

Principal Nodal Officer

The bank’s top grievance officer.

Internal Ombudsman

Reviews rejected or partly fixed cases. You do not file here first.

Housing finance

Branch or customer care

Where your written complaint usually starts.

Nodal or regional officer

Next if nothing moves. Listed by region on their site.

Grievance Redressal Officer

The HFC’s top complaint officer — sometimes head of CRM.

Complaint contents

Particulars and documents required for a valid complaint.

Who you are

Your full name, mobile, email, and address.

Your loan

Loan account number and branch.

What happened

The facts with dates. Any money involved.

What you want fixed

A refund, a correction, your papers, written confirmation, or compensation you can show with papers.

Send it by email, app, website form, or a letter they acknowledge.

Loan papers

Sanction letter and Key Fact Statement.

Your complaint trail

The written complaint and the acknowledgment or ticket.

Their reply, or silence

The final reply, or proof that 30 days passed with no answer.

Evidence pack

Statements, charge receipts, emails, SMS.

If papers are delayed after closure

Keep the loan-closed letter and proof you paid in full. Property papers should come back within 30 days. If the lender is late, RBI allows ₹5,000 a day. If papers are lost, the lender replaces them at its cost.

If someone files for you

You need an authorisation letter. An advocate can file with the Ombudsman only if that advocate is the person who was wronged.

Approach the regulator

When you can file, how to file, and what can be awarded.

When you may file with RBI

After the 30-day wait with no final reply — or sooner, if the bank already gave a final reply and the issue is still not fixed.

Deadline to file

Within 90 days of the bank’s last communication, or of the day the 30-day wait ended if there was no reply. Use the later of those two dates.

Before you file

Incomplete, anonymous, or suggestion-only complaints are not accepted. A request beyond what the Ombudsman can grant may be rejected.

Online

File through RBI CMS. Keep the complaint number.

Email or post

Send the complaint to CRPC by email or by post.

Helpline

Explains the process. Does not register the complaint.

Size of the dispute

No upper limit on the home loan dispute amount.

Consequential loss

Proven consequential loss capped at ₹30 lakh.

Time, expense, harassment

Additional compensation capped at ₹3 lakh.

If you receive an Award

Accept it in writing to the bank within 30 days, or it lapses.

Bank after an Award

30 days to comply, or to appeal where the scheme allows.

Your appeal

Certain Awards or rejections may go to the RBI Appellate Authority within 30 days, usually through CMS or the appellate email. Not every closure is appealable. Read the decision letter carefully.

File with NHB

File through NHB GRIDS, or use the complaint cell form on nhb.org.in.

Appeal at NHB

You may appeal to NHB’s Chief Grievance Redressal Officer within 30 days of the status intimation, as NHB states.

Exclusions

Matters outside this redressal path.

Wrong credit report

Start with the credit bureau and the bank that sent the data. Then RBI CMS if you still need help.

The bank refused the loan

This path will not force them to sanction it.

You only want a better rate

That is not a service complaint by itself. Compare and negotiate with lenders.

The same matter is already in court or a tribunal

A later Ombudsman filing on the same matter normally closes. Pick one main track.

Loan-linked insurance feels mis-sold

Take the policy side to the insurer and IRDAI. If the lender’s service around the sale is the problem, still write to the lender.

Fraud, bribery, or criminal harassment

Go to the police for the crime. Use bank vigilance for staff bribery. You can still raise the loan service side with the lender.

Consumer Commission, civil court, or Lok Adalat

Other legal tracks. National Consumer Helpline and CPGRAMS can sit beside this path.
