What is the complaint path?

Many issues get fixed with the bank or housing finance company if you complain there first.

If they don't resolve your issue, take bank complaints to the RBI and housing finance company complaints to the NHB.

Check who to escalate to first before regulator

Go up this list on the lender’s site when the first contact does not resolve it.

Bank

1. Branch or customer care

Logs your complaint and coordinates with the loan team.

2. Nodal officer

Regional officer who steps in if the branch fails to resolve it.

3. Principal Nodal Officer

Head-office executive who oversees complaints and liaises with the RBI.

4. Internal Ombudsman

Reviews rejected or partly fixed cases automatically. You do not contact them directly.

Housing finance

1. Branch or customer care

Logs your complaint and coordinates with the loan team.

2. Nodal or regional officer

Regional officer who steps in if the branch fails to resolve it. Listed by region on the HFC or NHB site.

3. Grievance Redressal Officer

Head-office officer whose contact the HFC must publish. Oversees escalations and tells you when you may go to NHB.

What must a complaint include?

Your name, loan number, what happened, and what you want fixed.

  • Who you are Your full name, mobile number, email, and address.
  • Your loan Your loan account number and branch.
  • What happened The facts with dates and any money involved.
  • What you want fixed A refund, correction, return of papers, written confirmation, or provable compensation.

Send it by email, app, website form, or a letter they acknowledge.

  • Loan papers Sanction letter and Key Fact Statement.
  • Complaint trail Your written complaint along with its acknowledgment or ticket number.
  • Lender's response Bank: their final reply, or proof that 30 days passed with no response. HFC: their final reply, or proof that one month passed with no response.
  • Supporting evidence Account statements, charge receipts, emails, and SMS records.
  • If the bank fails to return your papers within 30 days after loan closure Keep your loan-closure letter and payment proof. Property papers should arrive within this period. If delayed, RBI mandates ₹5,000 daily compensation; if lost, the lender replaces them at their cost.
  • If someone files for you You need an authorisation letter. An advocate can file with the Ombudsman only if that advocate is the person who was wronged.

When to escalate to the regulator?

After the lender's process, timeline rules differ: RBI governs banks, while NHB governs housing finance.

  • When to file After 30 days of receiving no reply from the bank, or sooner if the bank issued an unsatisfactory final reply and the issue remains unresolved.
  • Filing deadline Within 90 days of the bank’s last message, or 90 days from the end of the 30-day waiting period if no response was received—whichever date is later.
  • Pre-filing conditions Incomplete, anonymous, or purely suggestion-based complaints are rejected, alongside any demands that exceed the Ombudsman's jurisdiction.
  • Online submission File directly through the RBI CMS portal and retain your complaint reference number. cms.rbi.org.in↗(opens official RBI CMS page)
  • Email or post Submit your complaint documentation to the Centralized Receipt and Processing Centre (CRPC) via email or postal mail.(CRPC email: crpc@rbi.org.in)(CRPC post: Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre, Reserve Bank of India, Central Vista, Sector 17, Chandigarh 160017)
  • Helpline utility Use the helpline solely to understand the process, as it cannot register complaints.(RBI contact centre: 14448)
  • When to file After one month of receiving no reply from the housing finance company (HFC), or sooner if you received an unsatisfactory final reply.
  • Filing deadline Within one year of the HFC’s reply, or within one year and one month of your initial complaint if the HFC never responded.
  • HFC response timelines HFCs must send an acknowledgment within one week and strive to issue a final response within six weeks.
  • How to file Submit your complaint through the NHB GRIDS portal or use the grievance form available on nhb.org.in. grids.nhbonline.org.in↗(opens official NHB GRIDS page)
  • Appealing a decision Escalate your case to the NHB’s Chief Grievance Redressal Officer within 30 days of receiving your status intimation from the NHB.
  • Dispute size No upper limit on the home loan dispute amount.
  • Consequential loss Proven consequential loss capped at ₹30 lakh.
  • Mental agony Additional compensation for time, expense, and harassment capped at ₹3 lakh.
  • Accepting the award Accept the award in writing to the bank within 30 days, or it lapses.
  • Bank's timeline The bank has 30 days to comply or appeal where the scheme allows.
  • Appealing a decision You may appeal an RBI Ombudsman Award to the Appellate Authority within 30 days of receiving it. The Authority may allow up to 30 more days for a valid reason.(RBI appeal email: aaos@rbi.org.in)

When to Use Other Routes?

Certain issues require alternative channels instead of the standard Bank to RBI or NHB escalation route.

  • Loan refusals The Ombudsman cannot force a bank to approve your loan.
  • Interest rates Seeking a better rate is not a service complaint; you must compare and negotiate independently.
  • Wrong credit report Dispute the error directly with the credit bureau and the reporting bank first, then escalate to RBI CMS if unresolved.
  • Mis-sold loan-linked insurance Direct policy grievances to the insurer and IRDAI, while addressing any service failures regarding the sale back to the lender.
  • Fraud, bribery, or criminal harassment Report criminal acts to the police and staff bribery to bank vigilance, while handling any separate loan-service issues directly with the lender.
  • Active court cases Ombudsman complaints get dismissed if the same matter is in court; choose one path.
  • Other platforms Consumer courts and Lok Adalats are separate tracks, but the National Consumer Helpline and CPGRAMS can run alongside.(National Consumer Helpline: 1915) consumerhelpline.gov.in↗(opens official National Consumer Helpline page) pgportal.gov.in↗(opens official CPGRAMS page)
