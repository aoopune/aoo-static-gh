Two covers often sit
with the home loan.

Know what each is for before the cost joins the loan.

What does property cover pay for?

Property cover pays to repair or rebuild after listed damage.

Lenders ask so fire or flood does not wipe out the house that secures the loan.

See property cover

What does loan cover pay for?

Loan cover pays toward the outstanding home loan.

Lenders ask so the outstanding loan can still be paid if the borrower named on the cover dies.

See loan cover

Must you buy insurance?

Property cover is often required. Loan cover is usually optional.

  • Property cover Often written into the loan agreement while the loan runs.
  • Loan cover Usually optional.An existing term plan can often be used instead.

For both, you can usually choose the insurer — including one outside the bank’s partner list.

As per RBI, insurance is voluntary. Banks cannot force their insurer or tie it to the loan.

What to check before you buy?

Check the premium added to the loan, the refund policy if you leave early, and term plan vs. loan cover.

  1. Premium inside the loan If the premium sits in the loan, you pay interest on it too.Pay it separately and you avoid that interest.
  2. Early close or transfer Cover often stops with the loan, and you may get only some of the premium back, or nothing.
  3. Term plan instead of loan cover Often cheaper than bank loan cover for the same job. See loan coverto compare.
