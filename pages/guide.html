01

Loan amount

₹

Loan amount

Two limits apply. You receive roughly the lower one.

Property

The bank can lend only up to these percentages of the Agreement Value.

  • Agreement Value up to ₹30 lakh Bank can lend up to 90%.
  • Agreement Value ₹30 lakh to ₹75 lakh Bank can lend up to 80%.
  • Agreement Value above ₹75 lakh Bank can lend up to 75%.

These are the highest limits set by RBI.
Banks may approve a lower or higher amount depending on your profile and bank policy.

Income

The bank can lend only up to what your monthly EMI capacity supports.

  • Net take-home income per month
  • Less existing EMIs, and a share of your credit card limits
  • Whatever is left becomes your EMI capacity for the home loan.
  • From that EMI, rate, and tenure, the bank works out the loan amount.

All EMIs are usually limited to about 55–70% of take-home.
An earning co-applicant can help to increase the loan amount.

02

EMI

Equated Monthly Installment (EMI)

One monthly payment. Interest payment and principal repayment.

What you pay

Each EMI splits into interest payment on the outstanding loan and principal repayment that reduces the balance.

  • Early years: more of each EMI goes to interest payment
  • Later years: more of each EMI goes to principal repayment

EMI stays the same while the rate stays the same. If the rate changes, EMI or tenure may change.

When it starts

Depends on whether the home is ready or under construction.

  • Ready home: full EMI about one month after first disbursement
  • Under construction: pre-EMI (interest only) on the amount already disbursed
  • Under construction: full EMI usually starts around possession

Some lenders let you pay more than interest during construction. Ask if that option exists.

04

Rates

%

Interest rate

Two types. Banks may offer either.

Floating Preferred

Moves with the benchmark over the loan tenure.

  • External benchmark (often the repo rate) + the bank’s spread
  • Repo rate resets at least once every 3 months
  • If the rate rises, choose how the extra cost hits you: pay a lump sum, raise EMI, extend tenure, or mix these
  • Prepayment / foreclosure charges: None↗ (opens official RBI page) Part E, paragraphs 352 and 353

Fixed / hybrid

Fixed for a set period, then the rate resets.

  • External benchmark (often the repo rate) + the bank’s spread + a fixed rate premium
  • Usually fixed for 3 or 5 years, as per the lender
  • Usually about 1% to 2% higher than floating during the fixed period
  • Prepayment / foreclosure charges: 2% to 4%

After disbursement

After you take the loan, you still have choices.

You can switch between fixed and floating rates as per the bank’s policy, at the applicable cost.

You can switch between benchmark rates as per the bank’s policy, at the applicable cost.

If your credit score improves, you can ask to lower your rate as per the bank’s policy, at the applicable cost.

When the repo rate changes, an existing interest rate usually updates within about a month.

A newly sanctioned loan can take about 90 days for the first change.

05

Loan facility

Loan facility

Spare cash either cuts interest, or it does not.

Term loan Regular

Fixed EMI loan. Interest on the full leftover amount.

  • Money in your normal savings does not lower interest
  • To cut interest early, you have to prepay
  • Rate is usually 0.15% to 1% lower than the same bank’s overdraft
  • Fits if you will not keep large surplus parked for months
  • You win on rate if surplus will sit unused anyway

Overdraft

Home loan linked to an account. Interest drops when spare cash sits there.

  • Money lowers interest only if it sits in the linked account
  • You can withdraw that parked money when you need it
  • Rate is usually 0.15% to 1% higher than the same bank’s term loan
  • Fits only if you will actually keep surplus parked often enough
  • You win only if parked surplus saves more interest than the higher rate costs

After you choose

After the structure is set, these still matter.

You can switch between term loan and overdraft later as per the bank’s policy, at the applicable cost.

On overdraft, interest on the linked account is counted as per the bank’s policy.

On overdraft, you can withdraw parked money as per the bank’s limit and rules.

On overdraft, the usable limit may fall over the years (dropline), as per the lender.

If surplus rarely sits in the linked account, overdraft usually costs more than a term loan for no extra benefit.

The linked account is usually with the same lender.

On a term loan, spare cash still does not cut interest until you prepay.

06

Charges

Charges

Fees tied to getting the loan sanctioned and disbursed.

Bank fees

Fee the lender charges to process your case.

  • Processing fee Fee the lender charges to process your case. Part may be taken upfront as a login fee; the rest at final sanction.
  • Others Account handling fees or any other as per lender.

Other fees

Fees for checks and for registering the loan and security.

  • Legal and technical fees Fee to check legal papers and the property.
  • Title search report fees Fee to check the property title.
  • Valuation fees Fee to value the property.
  • Government charges Government fees to register the loan security.

Note — Lenders may bundle these charges, use different names, or charge them as one unified fee.

07

Project approval

Project approval

Banks check the project once instead of repeating the same checks for every buyer.

New or under-construction home

If a bank has already approved the project, phase, tower or wing, your home loan may move faster and reduce repeated legal, technical and search-report charges.

Resale home

For a resale home, the individual property is usually checked instead of the whole project. This may take additional time and include legal, technical and search-report charges.

Required before release: the property checks must be completed before the home loan is released.
