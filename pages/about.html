Our roots

Shroffs were India’s traditional indigenous bankers and money changers. They mark a real turn in the country’s financial history: from informal, community-based finance to organised, institutional banking. That was a moment when money in India became more structured, more systemised, and more central to economic progress.

Shroffin is here to advance banking itself. If shroffs were one stage in how banking evolved here, Shroffin is built for the next: banking that is more transparent, more informed, and more firmly aligned with the customer. The word carries India’s financial past. The work looks ahead to banking that can serve the world better.
