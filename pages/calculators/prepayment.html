How this estimate works

What goes into this estimate.

Formula used

Interest difference = interest on original schedule − interest after prepayment − fee

  • Balance after lump sum = outstanding − lump sum
  • Keep EMI — repay faster on the lower balance (tenure shortens)
  • Keep tenure — rebuild EMI on the lower balance for the same years
  • Each month still uses: interest = balance × r, principal = payment − interest
  • The starting EMI is rebuilt from the outstanding balance, rate, and years left.
  • The rate is held fixed after prepayment in this model.
  • Floating rate home loans to individuals usually have no foreclosure charge. Fixed rate loans may still charge a fee. Enter that fee if it applies to you.
  • Your bank may apply the lump sum on a set date in the month. Timing can change the exact difference.
  • This is not advice to prepay. It only compares the paths you enter.
