How this estimate works

What goes into this estimate.

Formula used

Max loan = Agreement Value × bank share

Down payment = Agreement Value − max loan

  • Bank share — RBI ceilings on Agreement Value: 90% / 80% / 75%
  • Agreement Value up to ₹30 lakh: bank can lend up to 90%.
  • Agreement Value ₹30 lakh to ₹75 lakh: bank can lend up to 80%.
  • Agreement Value above ₹75 lakh: bank can lend up to 75%.
  • These are the highest limits set by RBI. Banks may approve a lower amount depending on your profile and bank policy.
  • Stamp duty and registration are usually paid from your funds and are not financed here.
