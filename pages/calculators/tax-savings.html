How this estimate works

Rules follow the Tax benefits guide on this site.

Formula used

Section 22 interest claim = min(interest paid in year 1, applicable cap)

Schedule XV principal claim = min(principal paid in year 1, room left in ₹1.5 lakh basket)

Tax difference = tax(income before claims) − tax(income after claims)

Tax = slab tax + surcharge − Section 87A rebate + 4% Health and Education Cess

  • FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27 rates. New regime default slabs; old regime age-based exemption.
  • Surcharge and Section 87A include marginal relief at the relevant cliffs.
  • Let out: house property income ≈ annual rent × 70%, then less interest. Old regime loss set-off against other income capped at ₹2 lakh.
  • New tax regime slabs (FY 2025-26): nil up to ₹4 lakh; 5% on ₹4–8 lakh; 10% on ₹8–12 lakh; 15% on ₹12–16 lakh; 20% on ₹16–20 lakh; 25% on ₹20–24 lakh; 30% above ₹24 lakh. Section 87A rebate up to ₹60,000 when taxable income is up to ₹12 lakh, with marginal relief just above that line. Confirm on the Income Tax Department↗ (opens official Income Tax Department page).
  • Old tax regime slabs (individual): nil up to ₹2.5 lakh (₹3 lakh if 60–79; ₹5 lakh if 80+); 5% to ₹5 lakh; 20% to ₹10 lakh; 30% above ₹10 lakh. Section 87A rebate up to ₹12,500 when taxable income is up to ₹5 lakh.
  • Health and Education Cess is 4% on tax after rebate (including surcharge where due). Surcharge starts above ₹50 lakh of taxable income (10% / 15% / 25%; old regime can reach 37% above ₹5 crore), with marginal relief at each step.
  • Interest payment is modelled under Section 22. Self occupied old tax regime allows up to ₹2 lakh a year when the loan is to buy or build and the home is ready within 5 years. Otherwise the self occupied interest limit is ₹30,000 a year. Repair, renewal, or reconstruction is also ₹30,000 a year. Full map: Interest payment.
  • Let out interest has no fixed upper cap under Section 22. On the new tax regime, let out interest can reduce house property income; a house property loss usually cannot cut salary. On the old tax regime, house property loss set off against other income is limited to ₹2 lakh a year. See Other for loss set off.
  • Self occupied interest cannot be claimed on the new tax regime. See Interest payment.
  • Principal repayment is modelled under Schedule XV on the old tax regime only, up to ₹1.5 lakh a year in a shared basket. Same whether you live in the home or rent it out. It starts after possession. Full map: Principal repayment.
  • Year 1 figures assume possession has already happened. Pre possession interest claimed in 5 equal parts is not modelled here. See Under construction.
  • Extra first time buyer interest under Section 130 or Section 131 is not added. Those windows are narrow and closed for loans sanctioned after 31 March 2022. Details: Other.
  • Stamp duty, registration, insurance premiums in the Schedule XV basket, municipal taxes on rent, and special-rate income (for example some capital gains) are not modelled. Enter other Schedule XV use in the field above if needed.
  • Your name must be on the property papers to claim. Being on the loan alone is not enough. See How to claim and Joint home loan.
