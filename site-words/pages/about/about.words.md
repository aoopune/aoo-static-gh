## Story — Wanted to study abroad {#about.story.p1}
I wanted to study abroad. So I needed an education loan.

<a id="about.story.p2"></a>

## Story — Bike to every bank {#about.story.p2}
I took my bike and went to each bank on the street, one by one. Every bank had different terms, criteria, charges, and rates. There was no clean way to compare them.

<a id="about.story.p3"></a>

## Story — Agents and commission {#about.story.p3}
Agents and platforms were around. What I kept running into was thin information, uneven expertise, and recommendations shaped by bank commission. That creates bias and a conflict of interest. From their side, they are doing their jobs. From mine, I needed a view that was not steered that way.

<a id="about.story.p4"></a>

## Story — Built the spreadsheet {#about.story.p4}
So I started by myself. I built a Google spreadsheet of education-loan lenders across India — from conversations with bank executives, and from what was public on the internet.

<a id="about.story.p5"></a>

## Story — Book of information {#about.story.p5}
When I finished it, it still was not what I was looking for. It was a book of information, not answers for my own profile. I still had to sit and match my details to each bank by hand. That is when it became clear: collecting information is only half the problem. I needed answers for my own case.

<a id="about.story.p6"></a>

## Story — College visits {#about.story.p6}
To see how other students were doing it, I started visiting computer science colleges in Pune — PICT, VIT, MIT, COEP, Cummins, Symbiosis — and talking to people who were also planning education abroad. Many were stuck in the same place. I shared the spreadsheet link. I would see a new visitor on the sheet every hour.

<a id="about.story.p7"></a>

## Story — Apply Only Once {#about.story.p7}
The spreadsheet was asking for an interface — like a product. So I built a site where six inputs gave an apple-to-apple comparison of the banks, and you could apply in one go, with a guide to walk you through it. That was Apply Only Once.

<a id="about.story.p8"></a>

## Story — Full time {#about.story.p8}
Somewhere in that process, I realised this is what interests me. I cancelled my abroad plan, left my paying job, and started giving the project my full time.

<a id="about.story.p9"></a>

## Story — Home loans and Shroffin {#about.story.p9}
Abroad education loans are seasonal. Demand drops around March and April. That is when it hit me: home loans are the biggest secured lending market in India. A lot of people would be facing the same mess — and lending in general works the same way. So I did the work again. Collected bank data one by one. Built another sheet. Turned it into a product. That later became Shroffin.

<a id="about.story.p10"></a>

## Story — Day and night {#about.story.p10}
During the day, I speak with bank teams and keep the comparison data honest. At night, I work on the website. We are totally bootstrapped. My family helps cover basic living costs so the work can keep going. Our expenses are small, and we cover them from our own pockets.

<a id="about.story.p11"></a>

## Story — No commission, no fees {#about.story.p11}
We do not have a live revenue model today. We have one solid model we plan to put in place in a few months. What will not change: we do not take commission from banks, and we will never take commission that creates a bias or conflict of interest against you. And we will not charge customers a fee for as long as Shroffin exists.

<a id="about.story.p12"></a>

## Story — What we are trying to do {#about.story.p12}
What we are trying to do is simple. A fair view of home loans. Apply to the banks you choose, once, in one go. And leave the final decision with you — without commission steering the answer.
