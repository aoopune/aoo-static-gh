How this estimate works

What goes into this estimate.

Formula used

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

  • P — loan amount
  • r — monthly rate = annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100
  • n — tenure in months = years × 12
  • Interest is reducing balance with monthly compounding. That is the usual method for home loans in India.
  • The rate you enter is held fixed for the whole projection. Floating rates can change after sanction.
  • Fees, insurance, stamp duty, and Goods and Services Tax (GST) are not added into the EMI here.
  • The last instalment may be slightly different so the balance reaches zero.
  • A bank can still change EMI or tenure when the rate resets.
