How this estimate works

What goes into this estimate.

Formula used

P = E × (1 − (1 + r)−n) ÷ r

  • P — estimated loan amount
  • E — Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) you can pay
  • r — monthly rate = annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100
  • n — tenure in months = years × 12
  • This only reverses the EMI formula. It does not check income, credit score, or property rules.
  • Reducing balance and monthly compounding are used.
  • The rate is held fixed for the projection.
  • Your bank may sanction less after Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR), Loan to Value (LTV), and document checks.
