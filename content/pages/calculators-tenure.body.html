How this estimate works

What goes into this estimate.

Formula used

n = log(E ÷ (E − P × r)) ÷ log(1 + r)

  • n — tenure in months
  • E — Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI)
  • P — loan amount
  • r — monthly rate = annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100
  • Tenure is solved from the standard EMI formula.
  • If EMI is at or below monthly interest, no tenure can be shown.
  • Banks also cap tenure by age at loan end. That cap is not modelled here.
  • The rate is held fixed.
