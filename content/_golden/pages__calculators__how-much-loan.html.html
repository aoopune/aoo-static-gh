How this estimate works

What goes into this estimate.

Formula used

Property cap = Agreement Value × LTV (90% / 80% / 75% by price band)

Income EMI room = (combined take-home × FOIR%) − all existing EMIs − (10% × all card limits)

Income loan from that EMI room, then indicative loan = min(property cap, income loan)

Added by co-applicant = income loan with co-applicant − income loan without

  • Same two-limit logic as Guide · Loan amount
  • FOIR choices: 50%, 55%, 60%, 65%, 70% (Guide default 55%)
  • Two limits apply. You receive roughly the lower one.
  • Property: RBI ceilings on Agreement Value. Up to ₹30 lakh loan share 90%. ₹30–75 lakh 80%. Above ₹75 lakh 75%. Banks may still approve less.
  • Income: net take-home. Existing EMIs and about 10% of credit card limits come off first. What is left is EMI room for the home loan.
  • FOIR is bank practice, not an RBI fixed rule. An earning co-applicant’s take-home, EMIs, and card limits are combined under the same FOIR. Banks may still count less than 100% of co-applicant income.
  • Age, credit score, employer type, and document checks are not modelled.
