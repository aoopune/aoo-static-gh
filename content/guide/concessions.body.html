What can lower your home loan rate?

Some banks cut a little off the listed rate when you fit their rules.

  • Woman as primary applicant Often 0.05% to 0.10% lower. She is usually the sole owner or the first name on the property papers.
  • Salary with the same bank Your salary account with that bank can mean a lower rate.
  • Existing savings or loan customer Long-standing customers sometimes get a small cut at sanction.
  • Green home Often 0.05% to 0.10% lower when the project is rated by IGBC, GRIHA, LEED, or another approved Indian agency.

Your final rate is what the bank writes at sanction, not only the listed rate.

Who can get a PMAY subsidy?

A government cut on your home loan interest. The bank files it. PMAY (U)↗ (opens official PMAY page)

Who qualifies

  • Income band (EWS, LIG, or MIG) Household income and home price set your slab. Each has its own caps.
  • First home Usually no finished house already in your name anywhere in India. Many cases need a woman as owner or co-owner.
  • Loan within the subsidy cap The subsidy is worked out on a capped amount by category, not always on your full sanction.

How the money arrives

  • At loan sanction The bank checks eligibility and registers the claim with the nodal agency.
  • On your loan account It lowers your EMI or outstanding balance. You do not get cash in hand.
  • Aadhaar and income proof The bank files the claim. You do not apply to PMAY separately.

Can fees get waived?

Banks sometimes waive charges for a limited time.

During a festival or campaign

Processing fee may be zero or discounted. GST may or may not be included in the waiver.

Salary with the same bank

Processing fee may be waived when your salary is credited with that bank.

Offer with free legal or valuation

Those fees may be waived. Others still charge them separately.

Moving your loan to another bank

Takeover fees on the old loan plus fresh charges on the new one can cancel a small waiver.
