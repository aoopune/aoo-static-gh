What can lower your home loan rate?

Some banks offer a discount on their listed rate when you meet their eligibility rules.

  • Woman as primary applicant Often 0.05% to 0.10% lower. She is usually the sole owner or the first name on the property papers.
  • Salary with the same bank Your salary account with that bank can mean a lower rate.
  • Existing savings or loan customer Long-standing customers sometimes get a small cut at sanction.
  • Green home Often 0.05% to 0.10% lower when the project is rated by IGBC, GRIHA, LEED, or another approved Indian agency.

Your final rate is what the bank writes at sanction, not only the listed rate.

Who can get a PMAY subsidy?

If your household income is up to ₹3, ₹6, or ₹9 lakh a year, this is your first home, your loan is up to ₹25 lakh, and the house is up to ₹35 lakh. PMAY-U 2.0 ISS↗ (opens official PMAY interest subsidy page)

  • Income Household income up to ₹3 lakh (EWS), ₹6 lakh (LIG), or ₹9 lakh (MIG) a year.
  • First home You and your family should not already own a finished house anywhere in India. No housing-scheme benefit in the last 20 years. The house is usually in a woman’s name, or joint with her.
  • Loan and house Purchase, repurchase, or construction. Loan up to ₹25 lakh. House value up to ₹35 lakh. Carpet area up to 120 sqm.
  • Loan tenure Longer than 5 years. The 4% is worked out for up to 12 years.
  • Bank’s numbers Income and house value as the bank assesses them must stay within the caps.
  • Subsidy 4% a year on the first ₹8 lakh of the loan. Max about ₹1.80 lakh. Only for loans sanctioned and disbursed on or after 1 Sep 2024.
  • One house, one subsidy If a previous owner already got ISS on that house, you usually cannot.
  1. Register on the PMAY portal Put in your demand on the Unified Web Portal. Then it goes to your bank or housing finance company.
  2. On your loan account Subsidy comes in 5 yearly credits on the loan. It cuts principal. You do not get cash in hand.
  3. What you give the bank Aadhaar and income proof for the claim. Loan must stay active, and more than half the principal must still be outstanding when each credit lands.
  • Income bands EWS, LIG, MIG-I, MIG-II. Each band had its own income and loan caps.
  • First home No finished house already in the family’s name. Woman owner or co-owner in many cases.
  • Subsidy Interest cut on a capped loan amount by category (about 6.5% / 4% / 3% on up to about ₹6 lakh / ₹9 lakh / ₹12 lakh). Not always on your full sanction.
  1. Through the bank At sanction, the bank checked eligibility and filed the claim with the nodal agency. You did not file a separate PMAY form for that scheme.
  2. On your loan account It lowered EMI or outstanding balance. Not cash in hand.
  3. Closed for new loans MIG closed 31 Mar 2021. EWS and LIG closed 31 Mar 2022. Use this tab only if you already claimed CLSS, or your old claim was in that window.

Can fees be waived?

Yes, banks sometimes waive charges for a limited time.

During a festival or campaign

Processing fee may be zero or discounted. GST may or may not be included in the waiver.

Salary with the same bank

Processing fee may be waived when your salary is credited with that bank.

Offer with free legal or valuation

Those fees may be waived. Others still charge them separately.

Moving your loan to another bank

Takeover fees on the old loan plus fresh charges on the new one can cancel a small waiver.

For general understanding only. Concession rules depend on your income, property, and the lender’s current policy. Confirm PMAY on the official site and with your lender before you count on a subsidy. Banks and government schemes change over time.
