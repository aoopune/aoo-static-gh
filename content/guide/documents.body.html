01

Identity

Identity and Residence (KYC)

For applicant and co-applicant(s).

PAN Card Mandatory

Or Form 60, if the customer does not have a PAN Card.

Passport

Validity of which has not expired.

Driving Licence

Which has not expired.

Election / Voters Identification Card

Issued by the Election Commission of India.

NREGA Job Card

Issued by NREGA and duly signed by the officer of the State Government.

National Population Register Letter

Containing details of name and address.

Proof of Possession of Aadhaar Number

To be obtained voluntarily.

A document listed above shall be deemed valid even if there is a change in name subsequent to issuance, provided it is supported by a marriage certificate issued by the State Government or a Gazette notification indicating such change of name.

02

Income

Proof of Income

For applicant and co-applicant(s).

Salary Slips

Last 3 months.

Bank Statements

Last 6 months, showing salary credits.

Form-16 and IT Returns

Latest Form-16 and IT returns.

Employment Contract / Appointment Letter

If current employment is less than one year old.

Income Tax Returns with Computation

For at least the last 2 assessment years (individual and business entity), attested by a Chartered Accountant.

Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss Account

At least last 2 years, with annexures / schedules (individual and business entity), attested by a Chartered Accountant.

Bank Account Statements

Last 12 months’ current account statements of the business entity and savings account statements of the individual.

Business Profile

Business profile of the applicant.

Form 26AS

Latest Form 26AS.

Partnership Deed

Where the business entity is a partnership firm.

Memorandum and Articles of Association

Where the business entity is a company.

List of Directors and Shareholders

With individual shareholding, certified by a CA / CS — where the business entity is a company.

Details of Ongoing Loans

Of the individual and the business entity, including outstanding amount, instalments, security, purpose and balance loan term.

Keep about 6 months between each year’s ITR filing. Filing 2 or 3 years together just before applying can look like income made for the loan, not real ongoing income.

03

Property

Property documents

Pick the purchase type that matches your deal.

Allotment Letter / Buyer Agreement

Copy of the allotment letter / buyer agreement.

Payment Receipts

Receipt(s) of payment(s) made to the developer.

Occupancy Certificate

If the property is ready to move in.

Cost Sheet

Showing your total property acquisition cost.

Title Deeds

Including previous chain of the property documents.

Payment Receipts

Receipt(s) of initial payment(s) made to the seller.

Agreement to Sell

Copy of the agreement to sell (if already executed).

Title Deeds of the Plot

Proof of No Encumbrances

On the property.

Approved Plans

Copy of the plans, approved by the local authorities.

Construction Estimate

By an architect / civil engineer.

04

Other

Other documents

Own Contribution Proof

Receipts for amounts already paid, plus any valid document showing funds for the unpaid share.

Ongoing Loan Bank Statements

Last 6 months’ bank statements showing repayment of any ongoing loans.

Passport Size Photograph

Of all the applicants / co-applicants, to be affixed on the application form and signed across.

Processing Fee Cheque

Cheque for processing fee favouring the lender.
