### card: loan-amount
**On-page title:** How much can you borrow?
**Label:** Before you decide
**Confidence:** high
**Sources used:** overview.body.html; wb-rec-260815-2204; wb-rec-260815-2213/solution-04; startup-core

**Paste-ready intelligence**

The figure that usually wins is the lower of what the property can support and what your income can carry — not the higher of the two.

If that number feels thin, the honest levers are cleaner income papers or a real earning co-applicant who will sign. Inventing a name or stretching income on paper is how files die later, not how they get bigger.

The sanction letter is the amount that counts; the calculator here is only a sketch.

**Worked example:** none

- Guaranteed amount; fake co-applicant coaching

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout under the rule line.

### card: emi
**On-page title:** How do you repay the loan?
**Label:** Watch for
**Confidence:** high
**Sources used:** wb-rec-260816-0029; data overdue charges up to 0.24 (24% p.a.) on some lenders

**Paste-ready intelligence**

A slightly cheaper rate can still lose you money if one missed EMI is expensive — on our sheet, overdue interest on some lenders runs as high as about 24% a year.

Early years of an EMI are mostly interest, so a miss early hurts more than it looks. On under-construction, many people only pay interest on what’s disbursed until possession; if you have spare cash, it’s worth knowing whether that bank lets you pay a bit more in that stretch.

Start date and miss rules live on the Key Fact Statement — that’s the paper I’d read before locking the lender.

**Worked example:** none

**Must not say**
- Retell EMI principal/interest split encyclopedia
- Guaranteed lowest EMI

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout on the EMI card.

### card: tenure
**On-page title:** Up to what period can you repay?
**Label:** Before you decide
**Confidence:** high
**Sources used:** overview.body.html; wb-rec-260815-2204

**Paste-ready intelligence**

Longer tenure is comfort today and usually more interest over the life of the loan — you’re trading monthly ease for lifetime cost.

Brochures often say “up to about 30 years,” but your age can cut that short. Before you plan around the long number, it’s worth knowing the bank’s real end-age for someone like you.

When income rises later, shortening tenure is how a lot of people claw interest back — without gaming whose name is on the form.

**Worked example:** none

**Must not say**
- Restate the full tenure encyclopedia
- Put the loan in someone’s name only to game age

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout near the tenure calculator.

### card: rates
**On-page title:** What are the types of interest rates?
**Label:** Before you decide
**Confidence:** high
**Sources used:** data Floating 560 / Fixed 246; fixed_rate_premium often ~1–2%+; wb-rec-260816-0031; wb-rec-260815-2213 Flights honesty

**Paste-ready intelligence**

Most of what we list is floating — about 560 of 806 offers. That’s how the catalogue is stocked, not proof floating is “best” for you.

Fixed usually costs more on the way in (our sheet’s fixed premium often sits around 1–2% or higher) and can charge you to leave early. I’d take fixed only when a steady EMI for those years matters more than that premium — not because someone called it safer.

After sanction, a better credit score sometimes supports a rate conversation; the bank still decides. The Key Fact Statement is the rate story that sticks.

**Worked example:** none

- Guaranteed lowest rate; AI labels

### card: loan-structure
**On-page title:** What are the types of loan structures?
**Label:** Before you decide
**Confidence:** high
**Sources used:** data Term 505 / OD 301; overview OD ~0.15–1% higher; wb-rec-260815-2213

**Paste-ready intelligence**

Overdraft only wins if you actually park surplus cash against the limit often enough to beat its higher rate — on this Guide that’s often about 0.15% to 1% above the same bank’s term loan.

Most of our sheet is still term loans (505 vs 301 overdraft). If idle balances aren’t a real habit for you, term plus honest part-prepayment is usually the cleaner path.

If you do park large sums, ask the lender for both structures on the same amount in writing — including how dropline shrinks the limit over time.

**Worked example:** none

**Must not say**
- Retell what term vs OD is
- “OD always saves”

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout beside the structure tabs.

### card: charges
**On-page title:** What are the charges to get the loan?
**Label:** Watch for
**Confidence:** high
**Sources used:** wb-rec-260816-0004 (bank won’t take your report; Flights fee honesty); processing rows ~213, % up to ~3%

**Paste-ready intelligence**

Even if your own lawyer already cleared the title, the bank still runs its legal, title, and valuation — and it won’t accept your private report as a substitute. That second check is real money; knowing it early stops you paying twice for nothing.

Processing on our sheet can sit as a percentage (up to about 3% on some rows) or a fixed band. “Low fee” talk without the full list is the trap — some lenders sit cheap, some sit among the highest.

I’d match every rupee to the Key Fact Statement before login money leaves your account.

**Worked example:** none

- That your valuation will be accepted

### card: project-bank-approval
**On-page title:** Is your project bank-approved?
**Label:** Your move
**Confidence:** medium
**Sources used:** overview APF pattern; wb-rec-260816-0004

**Paste-ready intelligence**

For a new or under-construction home, an APF project often means less repeat legal and title work for that lender — which is where time and fee pain usually hide.

“All banks approved” on a brochure isn’t the same as that lender’s current list. I’d confirm APF with the named bank before heavy login fees, especially if you’re choosing between projects.

Resale is usually unit-by-unit anyway — expect a fuller property check there.

**Worked example:** none

**Must not say**
- Guaranteed faster sanction on every APF project
- Invented “all 33 banks” claims

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout near the project-approvals link.

**Explore handoff:** none
