### card: bank-rates
**On-page title:** What can lower your home loan rate?
**Label:** Before you decide
**Confidence:** high
**Sources used:** data women ~0.05% (4 banks); green 0.05–0.10%; wb-rec-260815-2213 honest spouse path

**Paste-ready intelligence**

The rate cuts that show up on our sheet are small — women-applicant about 0.05% at a few lenders, green-home about 0.05% to 0.10% when the rating is real — but on a long tenure that edge still compounds.

What doesn’t compound is a brochure “special” that never reaches the sanction letter. Fake a woman primary or a green certificate you don’t have and you’re not squeezing the bank — you’re risking the file.

I’d care about the rate written at sanction, not the perk poster.

**Worked example:** none

**Must not say**
- “All banks give women/green discount”
- Coach inventing eligibility

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout after the perk grid.

**Explore handoff:** Explore women/green tips when the filter is off.

### card: pmay
**On-page title:** Who can get a PMAY subsidy?
**Label:** Watch for
**Confidence:** high
**Sources used:** concessions.body.html PMAY-U 2.0; wb-rec-260815-2213 honesty

**Paste-ready intelligence**

PMAY isn’t a bank festival offer — it’s a government gate with hard caps on income, first home, loan size, and house value. Outside the band, the subsidy simply isn’t yours.

Credits cut principal over time; they aren’t cash in hand. I’d confirm on the official portal and in writing with the lender before building EMI math around it — and I wouldn’t invent income or hide an earlier house to squeeze in.

**Worked example:** none

**Must not say**
- Re-paste the full Who qualifies grid
- “You will get PMAY”; treat CLSS as open for new loans

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout on the PMAY card.

**Explore handoff:** none

### card: fees
**On-page title:** Can fees be waived?
**Label:** Watch for
**Confidence:** high
**Sources used:** wb-rec-260816-0004 Flights fee honesty; processing up to ~3%

**Paste-ready intelligence**

A waiver is only as good as what’s still due after it — GST, legal, valuation, and takeover costs on a switch can eat a “zero processing” headline.

On our sheet, processing alone can run up toward about 3% on some percentage rows, and it’s often payable if you proceed after sanction even when money never disbursed. Compare fees the honest way: some lenders sit low, some sit among the highest — believe the Key Fact Statement, not the flyer.

**Worked example:** none

**Must not say**
- Guaranteed waiver; “cheapest in India”
- Retell every festival story in full

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout after disclosures.

**Explore handoff:** Explore processing-fee gaps after Compare.
