### card: kyc
**On-page title:** What proves who you are?
**Label:** Watch for
**Confidence:** medium
**Sources used:** documents name-change callout; standing_practice; wb-rec-260815-2018

**Paste-ready intelligence**

Most KYC stalls aren’t missing a fancy ID — they’re one spelling or address that doesn’t match across PAN, photo ID, and the loan form.

Every co-applicant needs their own trail. Adding someone who isn’t really earning or owning just to “complete the form” usually creates a mess later, not a stronger file.

**Worked example:** none

**Must not say**
- Re-list allowed IDs
- Invent a co-applicant

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout near the name-change note.

**Explore handoff:** none

### card: income
**On-page title:** What proves your income?
**Label:** Before you decide
**Confidence:** high
**Sources used:** wb-rec-260815-2206 salary-slip path; wb-rec-260815-2213; hlc occupation tip — no fake salaried

**Paste-ready intelligence**

Banks don’t price the story you tell — they price the income path you can prove. Salaried and self-employed are different worlds on paper, not the same checklist with a new label.

If your file is thin today, waiting for a clean filing cycle with real papers usually helps more than dressing cash income as a salary you don’t have. A spouse only lifts capacity when that income is real and documented the same way.

**Worked example:** none

**Must not say**
- Re-list slip/ITR rows
- Fake salaried coaching

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout after the income tabs.

**Explore handoff:** Explore’s occupation tip after Compare.

### card: property
**On-page title:** What property papers do you need?
**Label:** Watch for
**Confidence:** high
**Sources used:** wb-rec-260816-0004 — bank won’t accept your own report

**Paste-ready intelligence**

Your sale papers start the file. The bank still re-checks the property itself — and it will not take a report you paid for privately as a substitute.

That second check costs money. Knowing it early is the save; finding out after you’ve already paid twice is the sting.

**Worked example:** none

**Must not say**
- Re-list New/Resale/Plot rows
- That a private lawyer letter replaces the bank check

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout after property-type tabs.

**Explore handoff:** Explore Charges for lender-side property-check fees.

### card: other
**On-page title:** What else might they ask for?
**Label:** Ask the lender
**Confidence:** medium
**Sources used:** wb-rec-260816-0004 processing fee honesty

**Paste-ready intelligence**

Processing fee is often due once you choose to proceed — even when a sanction letter already exists — and “optional add-ons” sold beside it are a different animal.

I’d want the Key Fact Statement and sanction list of charges in writing before any fee cheque goes out. Pay what’s named for the loan; push back on bundling you didn’t ask for.

**Worked example:** none

**Must not say**
- Re-list photos/contribution rows
- Guaranteed refund / “lowest fee”

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout before fine print.

**Explore handoff:** Explore processing-fee gaps after Compare.
