### card: path
**On-page title:** What is the complaint path?
**Label:** Your move
**Confidence:** high
**Sources used:** complaints.body.html; wb-rec-260815-2018 good-lawyer vibe; startup-core no villains

**Paste-ready intelligence**

A fair complaint has an order. Skip a step and the next door can close on you — not because someone is cruel, but because the system is built that way.

Talk, write, wait about thirty days, then RBI for a bank or NHB for a housing finance company. Branch people are doing their jobs; you’re climbing a process, not picking a fight with a person.

**Worked example:** none

**Must not say**
- That Shroffin files or wins the case
- Staff-as-villain framing

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout beside the path.

**Explore handoff:** none

### card: talk
**On-page title:** Where do you start?
**Label:** Your move
**Confidence:** high
**Sources used:** complaints.body.html; wb-rec-260815-2018

**Paste-ready intelligence**

A calm call with a ticket usually beats a loud visit with nothing in writing.

If they fix it, written confirmation closes the loop. If there’s no ticket, only vague promises, or the same issue returns, the same facts need to go in writing next — then up their own ladder on their site. Internal Ombudsman is a later review step, not the first desk you call.

**Worked example:** none

**Must not say**
- “Yell at the branch”
- Re-list every officer row as tips

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout on talk.

### card: write
**On-page title:** What must a complaint include?
**Label:** Before you decide
**Confidence:** high
**Sources used:** complaints.body.html; wb-rec-260815-2018 — papers that matter

**Paste-ready intelligence**

Short and dated wins a look. Who you are, the loan number, what happened, and what you want fixed — rants rarely do.

Sanction letter, Key Fact Statement, ticket trail, and proof of money or delay beside that letter is the pack that travels. One clear ask beats five mixed ones.

**Worked example:** none

**Must not say**
- That Shroffin drafts the letter
- Exact compensation you’ll receive

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout after letter/papers tabs.

**Explore handoff:** none

### card: stuck
**On-page title:** When do you go to the regulator?
**Label:** Watch for
**Confidence:** high
**Sources used:** complaints.body.html; startup-core — no outcome promises

**Paste-ready intelligence**

The regulator is for stuck files, not first contact. Clocks and routes decide whether a filing survives more often than anger does.

About thirty days with no final reply — or sooner once a final reply still leaves the issue open — is the usual gate, with a ninety-day window after that. Helplines explain; they don’t register. I’d open CMS or GRIDS only when the lender trail and dates already sit in one folder, and I’d recheck the official portal before trusting an old link.

**Worked example:** none

**Must not say**
- Guaranteed Award
- That 14448 registers the complaint
- Re-dump every money cap as a second encyclopedia

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout under the chapter rule.

**Explore handoff:** none

### card: limits
**On-page title:** What is outside this path?
**Label:** Watch for
**Confidence:** high
**Sources used:** complaints.body.html; startup-core honest limits

**Paste-ready intelligence**

This path fixes service and process failures. It doesn’t rewrite a bank’s credit decision or force a lower rate.

A refused sanction stays a refused sanction here. “I only want a better rate” is comparison and negotiation — Explore is closer to that job than Ombudsman. Wrong door burns the thirty-day wait on a form that was never going to help.

**Worked example:** none

**Must not say**
- That Ombudsman can force sanction or rate
- Villain framing of agents

**Fits existing UI as:** Soft callout under the rule.

**Explore handoff:** For better-rate-only, compare on Explore banks.
