## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

They switched to **Charges** (422942). On screen: **\*** on Processing fees and Property check, **^** on Government charges; fee amounts use dotted underlines. Raw ASR: “What is Dash? It is not Dash. It is indexation marking. … Dash is linking. If you click on it, it will take you there. Just notes. … I want a star. What does Apple do? They keep the markings underlined. So that they don't get confused.” Later: “Dash says that this is a link.” Then: “Actually there is an extra mark here. … There are three, right? Yes, there are three. And there are two stars.”

Join: **use a star for notes**; do not use a dash/link glyph; borrow Apple’s distinction between footnote marks and links; make the three indexes consistent (two stars + extra caret ^). That is proposed change + borrowed pattern + idea.

## What this is for

Charges column-title note marks and the Notes * / ^ blocks on Explore banks. Issue `issue-03-…` is the confusing glyphs; this file is the direction (stars, Apple, no extra caret).

## Why they said it that way

A dash (or dotted underline) tells people “this opens somewhere.” Notes should stay notes. Apple has many marks but keeps them from looking like links.
