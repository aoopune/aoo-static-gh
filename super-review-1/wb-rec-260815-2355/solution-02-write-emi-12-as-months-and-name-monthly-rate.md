## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

They clicked **₹37,938** (`Show how emi for Bank of Maharashtra was calculated`). Screen: **7.25% ÷ 12 = 0.6042%**, then EMI = ₹48,00,000 × **0.00604167** × (1.00604167)^**240** / ((1.00604167)^240 − 1) = ₹37,938.

Direction: **write 12 as months** and **name the monthly rate**. Raw ASR: “Oh, I need to know that interest is monthly. I don't know why I need to calculate the annual interest.” Then, clicking the 12 / formula spans: “12 is divided by months. It would have been better if it was written as months. And then why is it written into? What is 0.06?” (screen 0.00604167). They also said “Monthly interest rate into monthly interest rate compounded.” That idea is also the solution for this unlabeled formula. Showing EMI math at all is praised later (solution-03), not this file.

## What this is for

The EMI how-calculated drawer for Bank of Maharashtra on Explore banks. Issue `issue-02-emi-how-calculated-formula-unlabeled-12-and-0-006.md` is the unlabeled 12 / 0.006; this file is the direction to write months and name the monthly rate.

## Why they said it that way

They need monthly interest, not a yearly figure they must convert. The × in the formula (“into”) and the decimal 0.006 were unreadable without words.
