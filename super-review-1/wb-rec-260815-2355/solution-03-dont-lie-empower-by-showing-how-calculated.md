## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

After complaining the EMI formula was unlabeled, they praised **showing the explanation at all**. Raw ASR: “Very nice. Nobody knows how EMI is calculated. … This is a revolutionary product.” Then: “I don't think the agent knows this much.” They reopened loan amount: “How is the loan amount? I will show you the details. They give us a system. Or they give us details from our head office. … There is nothing like this.” On click: “I think if you click it, it will open immediately. Then it will slide and select. Very nice one.” Standing rule: “It is true that we don't lie to the customer. We are here to empower you.” Repeated: “We are here to empower you.”

That is company thinking / product principle, and an idea that can also be the solution: **keep (and improve) the how-calculated drawers so the customer is not left with a black-box agent number.** Labeling the math is solutions 01 and 02; this file is *why the drawers should exist*.

## What this is for

Shroffin’s stance toward the Explore banks customer: honest, empowering explanations of EMI and loan amount. Linked issue-02 only as the nearby defect; this file is not that defect.

## Why they said it that way

Trust: don’t lie. Role: empower. Contrast: house joke “we don't have money”; agent/head office has nothing like this chain.
