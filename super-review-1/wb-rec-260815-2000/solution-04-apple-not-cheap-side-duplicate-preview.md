# Apple-style — no cheap side-duplicate preview; show what you claim

Side-by-side accordion + repeating preview is “cheap”; many companies do it but Apple does not — even when kept, it is not normal for Shroffin.
When comparing, what is written on the accordion must match what the user actually sees — if it is written on top but not seen on top, they asked what the difference is.
The preview should show the real product (standardized view, not the same words twice), which they continue discussing in the next recording.

## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

They use **Apple** as the quality bar for Built around you’s preview panel:

- “It is cheap. Many companies do it. I have seen it. **Apple doesn't do it.**”
- “Even if they have kept it, **it is not normal**” for Shroffin.
- “I don't know how to do it… I can't do it” — they are stuck on how to show product properly, not endorsing the duplicate.

Company principle: **do not ship the generic marketing pattern** (accordion + mirrored blurb card). Show the thing.

Trust / comparison rule at the end:

- “But it is written on the top. It is not seen on the top. **What is the difference?**”

Joined to `screenshots/0053.png`: accordion row says “Every bank’s home loan in the same layout” while the card shows a small static table — not the live Explore banks product. The idea (also the solution direction) is: **when a row names a product capability, the preview must show that capability visually**, not restate the accordion sentence.
