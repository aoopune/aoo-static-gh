## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

An idea that can also be the motion solution: do not present two unexplained zeros. Stage the heading as they scroll. Option A: “zero commissions that appears first. And zero bias that appears later.” Option B: “just the first zero that appears first. And then as I scroll down, commissions. And then as I scroll down, even more bias. Or something of that sort.”

The two-zero broken look is the linked issue; this file is only that staging direction.
