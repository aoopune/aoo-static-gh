Change the words on “Zero commissions” so a customer does not hear “I pay you nothing.” Keep “Zero bias” in the same voice: the site talking to the customer. Imply the site does not earn from banks, has no bias, and does not push anyone — without teaching how Shroffin makes money, so people do not ask if you sell data.
This is for the finished Zero heading and the fair-view line under it.
They like the independence intent. They do not like how “commission” lands.
They were still hunting phrases when the recording ended (“zero commission, zero pay cuts”).

## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

This is both an idea and a proposed change, plus a standing company rule about trust.

1. Change the words on “Zero commissions.” A customer may think they pay no commission, or that Shroffin asks no commission from them, instead of “we earn no commission from the bank.”
2. Keep one perspective: the website talking to the customer. “Zero bias sounds good, but it should apply in the same context.” Do not pair website-to-bank “commissions” with website-to-customer “bias.”
3. Holding that customer voice constant: “how do we show that website does not earn anything? Website has no bias and website does not push anything. Find better words or better structure or better way to imply the same. Without letting the customer know that how do they earn? So that they don't question how they are earning.”

They like the intent: independent platform; don’t take commissions from anyone to push them; no inherent bias toward any bank. The fair-view line is the outcome they want to keep implying.

Trial phrases at the end (same hunt, not a second finding): “There is no word for pay on the listed platform.” “That we don't have to pay the bank.” “There can be zero commission, zero pay cuts.” Unfinished when the clip ended.

## Why they said it that way

Trust and how a customer reads the site. “Customer will always while reading the website, think that the website is talking to them.” If you say you earn nothing, “Most of the people think that we don't earn, then how do we earn?” “Do they sell data?” “I think they have some kind of trust. I had kept that in mind.”
