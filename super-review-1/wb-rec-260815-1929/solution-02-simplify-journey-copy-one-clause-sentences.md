## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

This is an idea that can also be the copy solution for the journey block: keep the four-beat story they like, but rewrite lines 2–4 into shorter, simple sentences (one subject, one predicate) so the same facts cost less effort to read, still in polished English.

They offered examples (quote audio.srt):
- Line 2: “Something like, see the entire market in one comparable view.” Also: “one standardized view that helps you compare rates, rules, and fine print across the entire market.”
- Line 3: replace crude “before you give your phone number” with “before we ask you for your details” or “before you are asked to surrender your contact information” or “before you need to give up some information.”
- Line 4: “choose your pick and apply in one shot, let them compete for you”; “Pick your banks and apply in one shot, and let them compete for you”; “Apply in one shot to your entire selection, and let them compete for you.”

Line 1 they called good. They did not pick a single winner among the examples.

## What this is for

The dark homepage journey region headed “We completely re-engineered your home loan journey.” Linked issue: `issue-02-journey-section-copy-too-complex.md` (the wordiness). This file is the rewrite direction and the floated lines.

## Why they said it that way

User convenience: “consuming a lot less context or brain power of the consumer that is reading it.” Grammar rule they stated: a sentence should not be too complex with multiple phrases; “one subject, one predicate as much as possible.” Phone-number wording “sounds a bit crude” and should become polished English.
