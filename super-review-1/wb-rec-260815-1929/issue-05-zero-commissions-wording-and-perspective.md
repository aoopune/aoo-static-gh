# “Zero commissions” is easy to read as the customer pays nothing

They like the intent: independent platform, no pay to push a bank, no built-in bias.
They do not think a customer will understand “commission” here — it sounds like “you pay us nothing,” not “we take nothing from the bank.”
“Zero bias” is fine only if it stays in the same customer-facing voice; they do not want the viewpoint to jump from bank to customer.
They want new words that still say no earnings from banks and no push, without making people ask how Shroffin makes money. That last point was still open when this clip ended.
