Write that this check is mandatory and that the customer’s own report will not be accepted.
That is so the customer is fully informed and can save money instead of paying twice.

## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

While Indian Bank’s property-check drawer was open they said the three steps (Legal and technical, Title search report, Valuation) are **mandatory**: the bank does them. **Even if you have already done them, the report will not be accepted by the bank.** The customer has to be **fully informed** (ASR “fully proofed”). They also said the first thing the site must do is help the customer **save money** — that is why this line belongs on the drawer, not only an industry-average disclaimer.

That idea is also the solution for the missing honesty on this charge.

## What this is for

Explore banks Charges → property-check drawer (opened on Indian Bank ₹13,300; same three-step copy later on SBI and Punjab & Sind). Issue `issue-02-property-check-copy-omits-bank-wont-accept-own-report.md` is the missing sentence. This file is the sentence they wanted.

## Why they said it that way

Brother-as-lawyer / title-already-clear is the customer story. Without the line, someone pays for a report the bank will throw away. Saving money is the company job.
