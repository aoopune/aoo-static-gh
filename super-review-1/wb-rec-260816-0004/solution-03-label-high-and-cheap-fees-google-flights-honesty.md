## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

They named **Google Flights** while sitting on a charge and said “this is one of the cheapest fees” — honesty like that service tells you prices are low. Then they opened high processing-fee banks and said **you have to write that these banks are one of the highest who charge processing fees.** On IDFC FIRST (3% of ₹48,00,000 = ₹1,44,000): **this bank is known for the highest amount of processing fees in the whole market** — **you can put it on the website.**

That is one direction: a cheap/high honesty layer on Charges, borrowed from Google Flights, which can also be the product intelligence (broker-like, because “intelligence has become cheap”).

## What this is for

Explore banks Charges processing-fee amounts and drawers (Canara cap ₹10,000, DCB 2% ₹96,000, IDFC FIRST 3% ₹1,44,000), and the same honesty for cheap fees. Issue `issue-03-high-processing-fee-banks-not-labeled-as-highest.md` is the missing “highest” label. This file is the Google Flights-style ranking they asked to write.

## Why they said it that way

A rupee alone does not tell the Indian user whether the fee is cheap or extreme. They said if they want to save they already know how (₹2,000 vs reality ₹10,000) — the site must still state the market-high truth. Broker / comfortable table / “intelligence has become cheap” is how they think Shroffin can sit with the user and give that callout.
