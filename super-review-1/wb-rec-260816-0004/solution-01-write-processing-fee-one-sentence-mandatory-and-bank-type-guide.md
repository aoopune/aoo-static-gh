## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

On Explore banks → Charges they treated the Processing fee * note as the place to answer the customer. They asked for **one sentence** defining the fee (ASR also offered “fixed or variable… for each successful loan disbursement,” which they then rejected). They said **write that this is a mandatory fee**, because the first question is “is there a way to avoid this fee?” They said **next write** private banks minimum fee and public banks minimum / variable fee — **a guide**. They repeated “There is no guide.” That direction is also the idea: the * note should be a short guide, not only a login-fee disclaimer.

They argued the fee is **not only on successful disbursement**. ASR “function letter” is treated as **sanction letter**: if you want to proceed you pay the full fee; it is non-refundable. Later they said “Processing fee. Variable fee.” while looking at high-fee banks — same object, not a second solution.

## What this is for

The Processing fee * header button and the Notes block “Processing fee (*)” on Explore banks Charges. Issue file `issue-01-processing-fee-star-note-missing-definition-mandatory-guide.md` is the defect (note only mentions bundled login fee). This file is what they said to write.

## Why they said it that way

The user’s first job is to understand the fee and whether they can skip it. They wanted “mandatory” written first so nobody hunts for a hack that does not exist. Private vs public minimum/variable is the second line of the same guide.
