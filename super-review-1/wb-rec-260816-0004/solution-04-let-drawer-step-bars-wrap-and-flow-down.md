# Let drawer step bars wrap so 1–2–3–4 flow down, as a shared layout

The numbered step cards in the charges drawers sit in a skinny column.
They said the bar is too small: if 1, 2, 3, 4 try to live in that width they split.
The fix they sketched: steps come down one after another with a line, wrap when width is less, and sit — the same repeatable layout on property-check and loan-amount drawers.
They said no to putting the standard on the side of the panel.

## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

They said the numbered **bar is small**. If 1, 2, 3, 4 try to sit in that width, **it will split**. Do not only “put one at a time.” **No** to keeping the standard on the side of the panel. Sketch: if 1–2–3–4 **came down**, 2 sits here, **put a line like this**, then you do not have to do each block independently. Open a small bar and give all the **sequentialities**; they will sit. **1, 2, 3, 4, 5, it will come down one by one.** If the width is less, that is okay — **it will flow.** They called this an **external, repeatable** thing after opening the loan-amount drawer with six steps.

That wrap/flow layout is the solution (and the idea) for every such drawer, not one bank.

## What this is for

Numbered step cards in Explore banks drawers: SBI property check ₹15,100; Punjab & Sind property check ₹14,700; Punjab & Sind loan amount ₹48,00,000 (six steps). Issue `issue-04-charges-drawer-step-bars-too-narrow-dont-wrap.md` is the crowding. This file is wrap-down-and-flow.

## Why they said it that way

A skinny stack is hard to follow. Wrapping with a connecting line keeps the sequence readable when the panel is narrow.
