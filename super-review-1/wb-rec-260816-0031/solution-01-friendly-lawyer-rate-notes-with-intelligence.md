## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

On Explore banks → Other charges, they opened Rate change charge Notes and rejected the current voice. Raw ASR (`audio.srt` cue 1): "I feel that all the symbols that I have to read, I have to use only legal language." Cue 2: "Versus it should be like a friendly lawyer who is in your team to help you out." Cue 4–5: "For example, look at this. MCLR, BPLR, these are the things." / "So here instead what I want is how the rate is decided."

The constructive direction — also an idea for Shroffin's intelligence layer — is:

1. **Voice:** Notes should explain how the rate is decided and when the customer should choose, not force legal benchmark names (MCLR, BPLR, benchmark-switch jargon).
2. **Customer benefit framing:** "I want to know what is the benefit for me, or when should I choose it as a customer." (`audio.srt` cues 15–16).
3. **Scenario advice (replace legal symbols):** If you don't have much stress, go with RBI/repo-linked floating (ASR "RBI report"); if you feel the market or economy is going down or rates will rise, consider fixed — fixed can be ~0.25% higher and that is already shown on screen (cues 9–13, 19–25).
4. **Shroffin intelligence:** "When our intelligence would suggest that if India is going down, then you should not take a fixed rate — floating rate is going to be a favorable term in the long term." (cues 28–30). This is both product intelligence and note copy.
5. **Same note pattern elsewhere:** "Ok, this is again the same, right?" / "Yes, it is the same." — Overdue and EMI bounce notes should use the same friendly-lawyer voice (cues 31–35).

They also want five or six external-benchmark "agencies" (ASR "HR"; on-screen notes discuss repo/RBI-linked rates) with **no advantage or disadvantage baked in** — the customer chooses what fits them (cues 6–8).

Explore banks → Other charges → **Notes**, especially `details#hlc-charge-note-rate-change-charge` opened after clicking header mark `°` on Rate change charge.
