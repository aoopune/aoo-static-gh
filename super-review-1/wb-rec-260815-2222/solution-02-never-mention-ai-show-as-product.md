# Never mention AI — show intelligence as product

When Explore banks shows advice or recommendations, never label it AI-driven — zero mention of AI to the customer.
Where intelligence appears, present it as ordinary product output (“our recommendation engine”), the way Netflix or Postgres never advertise their machinery.
They disagreed on wording: saying “AI driven company” cost trust vs their CA-driven positioning; people trust the CA’s basis over a prompt agent.
This rule applies to the top-three tips feature and any future intelligence on this page.

## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

**Customer rule:** “should we show them that this is all AI driven? **No, no. Zero.**” “What we use in engineering is **not a concern of the customer**.” Do we say we use Postgres? No. “**It is our recommendation engine.**”

**Presentation rule:** “There is only one problem. where intelligence is shown… basically **it is a product**. **Nothing is AI. We never mention AI.**”

**Trust basis:** “if AI is saying something and **the actual CA** is saying something, then people will trust **the actual CA's information**.” Prompt/agent answers → “I don't trust them.”

**Borrowed negatives:** Good companies (Netflix: “We think you will like this”) don't say machine learning. **Disagreement:** “Shut up, shut up. You are an AI driven company… **On the contrary, I have lost my trust.** You said you are a CA driven company.”

**Competitor warning:** Birbal / AI tele-calling — funding goes to AI companies; “That guy is a fool… if he uses AI”; don't copy “we are AI” for funding. “**No one can say that they are AI**” about accurate tips.

## What this is for

How **Explore banks** (and Shroffin intelligence generally) should **read to the customer** when showing optimizations, tips, or recommendations — linked to the missing tips feature in issue-01.

## Why they said it that way

Shroffin is **CA-driven**, not an AI startup poster. Customers trust **substance and basis**, not stack labels. Competitors who lead with AI erode the same trust Shroffin needs for lakh-saving advice.
