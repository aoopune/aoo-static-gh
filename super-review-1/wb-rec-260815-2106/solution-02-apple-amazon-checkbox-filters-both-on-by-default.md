Same for Floating/Fixed and Term loan/Overdraft — make the block uniform checkboxes; Floating selected by default.
Decided direction; they were confused by exclusive one-at-a-time pills.

## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)
On Explore banks Filters, use **checkboxes** like Apple/Amazon: **Public and Private both ticked by default**; **All is not a separate filter**. Apply the same to **Floating/Fixed** (Floating selected by default) and **Term loan/Overdraft** — “Then it becomes uniform.”

## What this is for
Filters sidebar: **Bank type**, **Rate**, **Facility** controls while comparing the bank table.

## Why they said it that way
Exclusive pills confuse (“What is this button?” “All the checkboxes.”). Multi-select matches how they filter elsewhere.

## How the files join (required)
- time: **106220–203600 ms**
- said: “Both are ticked by default. All is not a separate filter.” / Apple/Amazon / “Why do we need a separate button?” / “And the same here… uniform.”

- screen: `0015.jpg` Public only; `0018.jpg` Private only empty table; `0035.png` All+Floating+Term loan
- therefore: replace exclusive pills with default-both-on checkboxes

## Pinpoint
Apple/Amazon checkbox multi-select for Bank type, Rate, and Facility — Public+Private both on, no All pill.

## Related discussion (not the solution itself)
