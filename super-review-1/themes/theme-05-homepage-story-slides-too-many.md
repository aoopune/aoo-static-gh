## Exact theme

The homepage story is a stack of full-screen slides (Transparent like never before; Zero commissions / Zero bias; Best of all / own pace; then Built around you and other unique-point screens). After walking Zero and Best of all they asked: “Now, I have a concern that these four sections do we need these four sections?” “Can we trim some of these sections?”

In the next recording they treated the slides as too many and too thin. Quote: “Okay, we are transparent, but I don't think we should do an entire slide.” “Zero commission, zero bias. We have already said this.” They counted six unique points and asked to refactor into fewer slides — two, then three, then the last — “The goal of entire activity is consolidation” without making the homepage longer.

Later they clicked through Built around you, mapped rows back to unique points already covered, named “Help toward what you need” as a seventh point whose sentence they could not parse, and said to scrap Scattered everywhere as a section and rebuild the four dark sections so seven points flow in few polished words.

This is story architecture (too many thin slides / points restated), not the wording of Zero commissions, not the two-zero scroll, and not Built around you’s accordion/preview controls.
