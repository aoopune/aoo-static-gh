## Exact theme

The joined attack is on **sentences that make the reader work**, or that **do not earn their place**.

On the dark journey block they like the four-beat story (re-engineered journey, whole market in one view, look before sharing a number, pick banks and they compete). They still want the same facts in fewer, simpler sentences. Quote: “But I feel we can convey the same amount of information using a lot less words by keeping the English polished still.” “It should be simple, one subject, one predicate as much as possible.” “Before you give your phone number sounds a bit crude.” Later they kept the title and three parts but said: “This is definitely a coherent story. But the word or the sentence formation is not that coherent.”

On Transparent they like the big line and reject the small one. Quote: “So you find what works for you, I don't think it adds so much value.” “And with no surprises, I don't think it adds any value at all.” They would find sentences that add value, skip the section, or redesign it.

On Best of all they asked what spam calls, a hard sell, and pushy notifications are for, and how they differ. Quote: “Spam call and hard sell are a bit intersected.” The point they wanted was no urgency — look at your own pace, no extra calls, no buy/sell nags.

Later, still applying the same bar, they said extra “no”s and a long CTA raise cognitive load: “No x, no y, no z. This cognitive load has increased.” “Compare all and apply in one shot. And banks will compete for you. The load has increased.” Lighter: “Compare and apply. Let banks compete.” On Explore banks they called “Data last checked on 14 July 2026” not correct English and asked for polished English such as “Accuracy guaranteed” or “updated as of.”
