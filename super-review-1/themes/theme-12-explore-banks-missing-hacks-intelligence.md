# Explore banks lists today’s banks but does not give the hacks

The page collects today’s numbers and lists matching banks.
They said that is the wrong job. They want change-this-to-save-that advice — wait, raise CIBIL, take it in a wife’s name, get a salary slip — without stuffing it into the table so the rows go approximate.
They wanted a Google Flights-style honest layer, three pre-search tips, and top-three optimizations over months to a year, not labeled AI.
The first land should already show average values, then intelligence, then the list, obvious at a glance. Linked fields also fail to carry the first inputs or show ROI first.

## Exact theme

Explore banks currently asks for present figures and lists banks. They treated that as the wrong product job.

Quote: “Actually, I don't want you to tell me what the best parameters should be.” They want what a person could change — wait months, raise the score, salaried vs self-employed, put a younger wife’s name on the loan — and how much that saves. Quote: “If you have taken your wife's name, then you have saved Rs. 400,000.”

Next: Indian users want hacks that save lakhs. Quote: “Instead, I want that if this company gives me that intelligence,” “I need to know these hacks with this tool.” “But this place is empty.” Putting that advice into the same bank list would mess up the table below (“The things below, They get approximated.”).

They debated a different tool vs the same page. Quote: “Bro, we need to make a different tool.” Then: “We don't need two interfaces.” They wanted Google Flights-style honesty about rates, three tips before search, occupation tips, and “Give me the best.”

Then the missing feature: top three optimizations so a user can reach a better offer over months to a year. Quote: “You suggest top three optimizations that the user can reach the best offer in the next few months to one year.” “should we show them that this is all AI driven? No, no. Zero.”

First land: average values already filled, intelligence appears, then the bank list, automatically. Quote: “And you have to fill in the average intelligence in the first shot.” “The intelligence comes and then the list comes.” At a glance they wanted the filled form, three tooltips, and at least two table rows together. Quote: “At one glance, you will know how this page was made.”

Linked fields: you cannot keep all of the first information. Put income, then change property and age; monthly income falls and ROI changes, but ROI is not shown first. Quote: “You can't take all the pieces of the first information.” “ROI will change. But before that, tell me what is the ROI?” “We need two tips.”
