## Exact theme

On the Shroffin home page, the first block they looked at is the top hero: the heading **Get a fair view of home loans…** and the **Explore banks** button. They said the amount of space above that text and below the button is not even, so the text and the button do not feel as if they sit in the center of the whole block.

Quote: “First thing I see in this top block, which has the Get a Fair View of Home Loans, this text, is that the amount of spacing above this text and below the CBE is not even.” Then: “It should feel like the text and the button are in the center of this entire block.” (ASR “CBE” is the Explore banks button.)

This is a spacing problem in that top stack, not a complaint about the video or the photo behind the mockup.
