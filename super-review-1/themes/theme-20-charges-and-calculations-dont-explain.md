# Charges and calculations do not explain themselves

Exact rates and odd loan amounts print without a star, so the site does not stand behind the number.
How-calculated drawers show bare percentages and formulas (80%, 12, 0.006) without names, and step bars are too narrow to wrap.
Charge notes mix legal words (MCLR, BPLR), footnote marks that look like links, missing definitions, no rupee math for a missed EMI, and no “highest” labels.

## Exact theme

Money on Explore banks is shown as exact figures, legal notes, and unlabeled steps — not as something a customer can follow.

They said the table has taken an exact amount without a star (8.75%, ₹47,92,101). Quote: “They have taken an exact amount without a star.” “So I will put a star.” “At least stand behind something.”

How loan amount is built: they liked following 1–5, then said it is not neat. Quote: “It's not just neat.” “If you have a color code, it will show you.” “Minimum of 2.” Next clip: 80%, 55%, 10%, −₹555 have no names. Quote: “So I don't know why the next step is useful.” “I need their names and labels.” EMI formula: 7.25% ÷ 12 and 0.006 unlabeled. Quote: “It would have been better if it was written as months.” “What is 0.06?” Step bars in drawers are a skinny column that does not wrap. Quote: “And this bar is small.” “If it is the width, 1, 2, 3, 4, it will split.”

Charges notes: a dash/caret looks like a link; they want stars. Quote: “What is Dash?” “I want a star.” Processing fee * never defines the fee, never says it is mandatory even if the loan is not disbursed, and has no public-vs-private guide. Quote: “So, first I want to know what is a processing fee.” “There is no guide.” Property check never says the bank will not accept a report you already paid for. Quote: “Even if you have done it, the report will not be accepted by the bank.” High-fee banks (Canara cap, DCB, IDFC FIRST) are not labeled among the highest. Quote: “And you have to write that these banks are one of the highest who charge processing fees.”

Government charges show the same rupees on every bank and repeat in the side card. Rate-change notes are MCLR / BPLR legal talk, not friendly-lawyer advice. Quote: “Should feel like an advice coming from a lawyer instead of just technical terms and legal words.” Overdue is 2% / 5% / 8% p.a. with no rupee for one missed EMI. Quote: “Show me the calculation.” Other charges has no clickable drawer for extra money including bounce. Banks are not labeled lowest/highest EMI-miss penalty (J&K vs IndusInd).
