## Exact issue
On Explore banks, site copy and chrome still say **banks** (page title “Explore banks.”, filter **Bank type**) while the results column is already labeled **Lenders** and the rows include housing-finance names. They treated “banks” as the wrong / too-narrow word for a list that includes NBFCs, and asked where the **neutral** word (lenders) should go versus bank-only wording.

## How the files join
From ~00:42 to ~03:36 they sat idle on the filled loan form (`0007.jpg`–`0027.jpg`, identical view). Speech: “Right now, we have banks. Lenders. If we talk about lenders, we don't even have banks. But we have lenders everywhere.” They recalled older copy: “get a fair view of home loans and apply to your chosen banks.” Then NBFC / housing company; some people think everything is a bank; knowledgeable people know lenders and banks. “Where should we use neutral? Neutral means lenders, right? … Bank specific bank. All loan options.” Screenshots of the form do not show the table column; they had already seen **Lenders** on `0001.jpg`. No click during this talk.

## Pinpoint
Explore banks — calling the product and filters “banks” while the comparison is lenders (including NBFCs) is the wording mismatch they were deciding.

## Related discussion
