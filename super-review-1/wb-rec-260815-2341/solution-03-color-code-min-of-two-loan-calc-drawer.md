## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

They opened **Show how loan amount for Bank of Maharashtra was calculated** (₹48,00,000) and kept the Loan amount drawer open from t=376787 through session end.

They praised the numbered walk: “It is good. It is definitely good. You just have to follow 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.” Walking steps: 48 lakhs, 55,000 income allowance, credit-card load — “This information is great.” Screen shows six cards: Property limit 80%, Income allowance 55%, Credit-card load 10%, Monthly EMI available, Income limit with **240 months**, Lowest of these limits ₹48,00,000 vs higher income-limit figure.

Then layout attack: “It's not just neat. If you have a color code, it will show you.” Repeated. “Look, bro. Minimum of 2. Then left-side calculation, right-side calculation. 1-2-3, 1-2-3. Minimum of 2.” They agreed “Yes, it's correct” — math is right; presentation is not neat enough.

Tenure hunt: ASR “2.40 months” while screen shows **240 months** in step 5 and **20 years** in table/adjust panel — “Where is 2.40 months? 20 years.” Settled: “Yes, this is 20 years.”

Session ends still on this drawer arranging limits (“20% … that's the limit”; “First, this is 25. Then this is 1 lakh.”).

**Proposed change:** color-code two parallel 1-2-3 calculation columns (property path vs income path) so step 6 “Lowest of these limits / minimum of two” reads visually — not a math change.

They want transparency without forcing the customer to parse a vertical stack — “what about this? I don't know.” Color groups the two competing limits so the minimum-of-two moment is obvious. Praise for follow-1-5 shows they keep the numbered steps; neatness/color is additive.
