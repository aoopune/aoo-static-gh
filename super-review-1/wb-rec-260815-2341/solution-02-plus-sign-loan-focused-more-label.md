# Use a compact plus sign and loan-focused label for extra info on the bank row

The extra-info control on Bank of Maharashtra should not rely on an underline nobody can guess — use a small plus sign instead.
It should not be big, not dominate the phone layout, and the column should stay narrow.
The label should say more about the loan, not “More about Bank of Maharashtra,” because a Super Housing Loan customer needs loan/scheme details.
When people are told what is inside, they click — so the cue and label must match the dump vs the calculation drawer.

## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

After closing More details they looked at the Bank of Maharashtra row on the table. They said the bank “didn't underline it” and “put a clip in it” — “How to guess underline?” They brainstormed “ADD.info” twice and constraints: “It should not be big. It should not be on the phone. Column should not be big. You have to put the plus sign.”

They contrasted two clickable patterns on the same row: underline on **loan amount** opens “more calculations” (loan-amount how-calculated drawer from ₹48,00,000); the **More** control opens “the dump of all information.” They said both are clickable but it should not be a big deal to tell them apart.

On naming: “If you want to know more about the bank of Maharashtra, click it. But if you take the super housing loan, I think you should know more about the loan.” — “It is a little misleading.” Events show accessible name **More about Bank of Maharashtra** with visible **More**. They said it is “not standard,” they “used to write more details,” and “If you write something, it is more” — too many “more”s.

User-convenience rule they stated: “Generally, when people are told what is inside, they click it.”

Constructive package: compact **plus** cue (mobile-safe, narrow column), honest **loan/scheme-focused** label for the dump, and clear separation from the loan-amount calculation underline.

## What this is for

Explore banks compare table — Bank of Maharashtra row extra-info affordance (More button, possible underline/plus/clip) vs ₹48,00,000 loan-amount control. Related issue: `issue-02-more-cue-underline-plus-misleading-bank-label.md`.

## Why they said it that way

Discoverability on a dense table; mobile column must stay small; misleading “about the bank” wording when the drawer is loan/scheme static info; customers click when they know what they will get; two similar patterns should not feel like the same job.
