# Put bank scheme facts in the product, not only in More details

The Maha Super Housing Loan scheme facts are good — a customer could take them to a branch manager and say “this is your scheme.”
Those facts should live in the Shroffin product itself, not only inside the More details drawer and not as a blog.
Other banks put a dedicated page per bank; they said they will talk about that page later.
More details is fine as a dump, but the same static information should also be out in the product.

## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

They praised the Scheme block in More details — “No, this is definitely good information” — then role-played a customer who does not want Super Housing Loan but still needs to know “what is your scheme?” They said Bank of Maharashtra has no answer, but the customer can tell the manager “this is your scheme.”

They added a reach problem: if someone already thinks their loan is under some other scheme, they will not open this dump and will miss the facts; only a customer who does not know will find it. “It is very important.”

After walking other accordions they called it static information. “How do other banks do it? They put a page in each of the other banks. We will talk about that page. But, not as a blog. It is an information. But, it should be out there. It should be in the product.” On More details itself: “It is good… It is not important. But, it should be in the product. It should be in the same product.”

The constructive direction is not to delete More details. It is to surface the same scheme/bank static facts in the main product — per-bank page model, information not blog — so scheme identity is findable even when the customer never opens the drawer or already believes they are on another scheme.

## What this is for

Bank of Maharashtra · Maha Super Housing Loan scheme identity (bank, scheme name, facility, purpose, rate type, borrower category) on Explore banks. The related issue (`issue-01-scheme-facts-only-in-more-details.md`) names the defect; this file is the direction they gave.

## Why they said it that way

They want customers armed for a branch conversation, not stuck because the bank itself cannot name its scheme. They treat scheme identity as important static information that should not depend on opening a drawer or on the customer not already having a scheme belief. Borrowed pattern: other banks expose a page per bank; they explicitly rejected blog framing.
