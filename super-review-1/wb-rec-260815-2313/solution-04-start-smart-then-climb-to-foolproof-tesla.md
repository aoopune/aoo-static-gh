Shroffin should first serve the sharper people who really need this, then slowly make the product foolproof for everyone else.
This is company thinking about who the site is for at launch, discussed while sitting on Explore banks.
They used Tesla going to mass market as the pattern, and said do not worry if early traffic is smart people who need it.
They spent a long aside spelling foolproof (not “full-proof”), like waterproof.

## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)
They imagined the majority of the public as “stupid,” with a shortage of people who take realistic decisions. The maximum who come, including the non-realistic, still have to be impressed. Attention to detailing does not impress them. Climb their ladder: start with most non-stupid, gradually come to stupid, make the solution foolproof. Then slowly become foolproof. You don’t have to do full appreciation, but you have to appreciate it. How did Tesla go to mass market? Don’t worry as long as we get some traffic and some smart people who really need it. This idea can also be the launch principle: first the people who need it and get it, then foolproof for the rest.

## What this is for
Company / launch audience, not a single button. They were idle on Explore banks with the Existing EMIs tooltip still open. No matching issue file; this is company thinking.

## Why they said it that way
Details on the form will not win the mass. Tesla is the borrowed path to mass market. Early smart traffic is enough to start.
