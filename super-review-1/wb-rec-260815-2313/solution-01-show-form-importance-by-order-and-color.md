# Show what matters most with order and color

Filling the loan form should itself teach which questions matter most, without anyone explaining.
This is for the Loan inputs card on Explore banks.
They wanted top-to-bottom to mean importance (how much money, then rate, then tenure) and left-to-right to mean column importance, using colors, sequence, and order.
They said it may not be the most urgent change right now, but it should still be done.

## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)
They treated this as both an idea and a change to make: while filling this form the user becomes intelligent. They should not need an explanation. They should directly understand that an outcome happens due to property value, and that tenure is the most important property. Top to down: what is most important — how much money, how much interest rate, how much tenure. Left to right: the importance of the column. Do that with colors, sequencing, and order. If applied, the form will change. Maybe not most important at this moment. But it should be done. Later they restated the same reading habit: people usually train left to right, up to down; we do the same.

## What this is for
The Loan inputs form and the bank-table columns (Rate, Loan amount, Tenure) on Explore banks. Issue file `issue-01-loan-form-importance-not-shown-by-order-color.md` is the defect (nothing on screen ranks importance). This file is the direction.

## Why they said it that way
So the form itself trains the user. They joked the other person thinks they will be “hit” if they have to explain. They later said people are already trained left-to-right and top-to-bottom, so the site should follow that.
