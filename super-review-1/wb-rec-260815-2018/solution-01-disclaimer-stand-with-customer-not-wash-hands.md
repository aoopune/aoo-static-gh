## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

While the home footer **Disclaimer** was expanded on screen, they treated the block as something Shroffin should **rewrite**, not delete.

They want to **keep the legal limits** — Shroffin is a third party, not a bank / NBFC / lender (ASR: “letter”; on-screen: **lender**), and they do not approve, sanction, underwrite, or disburse loans. Raw ASR while reading the panel (`audio.srt` 01:38.880–02:04.180): “We do not approve sanction underwrite or disbursed language… Shroffin is not a bank, NBFC or letter… We do not approve sanction underwrite or disbursed loans.”

What they want changed is the **voice**. They asked whether Shroffin can sound **different from everyone else** (`audio.srt` 01:15.400–01:19.380): “But everyone is like this. Can we do something different?”

They then **drafted replacement direction** out loud (`audio.srt` 01:20.500–02:36.380), still on the expanded disclaimer:

- Say they are not really responsible because they are third party, **“But we try a lot.”** / **“We try to work within your maximum benefit.”**
- Frame Shroffin as **“a platform that standardizes third party offerings into views that are easy for customers to comprehend.”**
- Say what they **try** from data and interactions: how banks may underwrite and disburse for the customer; guide in the financial journey.
- **“We are not taking responsibility. But we are telling them that we try our best to take the responsibility. But ultimately it's in your hands.”**

They contrasted site vibe vs disclaimer vibe (`audio.srt` 02:46.300–02:59.520): the website so far feels like **“we are there for the customers”**; the disclaimer feels like **“we are not responsible for anything.”**

The **desired vibe** (`audio.srt` 03:00.680–03:37.240): although legally not responsible, Shroffin should feel it is their responsibility to be **true, honest, and work in the best interest of the customer**; language should **not** feel like **“legally doing away with obligations”** but like **“although legally we are not responsible, we try our best to make it so that you have the best way possible dealing with them.”**

**Borrowed pattern — good lawyer** (`audio.srt` 03:37.460–04:01.360): “It is like as if you are dealing with a good lawyer. Lawyer is not responsible for what case or judge will decide… But a good lawyer is there by your side to guide you through that entire journey… But a good lawyer inspires confidence in you.” Applied here to how the disclaimer should feel.

Bridge line before the next topic (`audio.srt` 04:07.760–04:13.900): “Even if you don't understand, we have to put our words into it.”

## Why they said it that way

- **User convenience / trust:** A first-time visitor should feel Shroffin is on their side at launch, consistent with the rest of the homepage story.
- **Company thinking:** They are a platform and guide, not the lender — but they still try hard for the customer’s benefit; ultimate decision stays with the customer and the bank.
- **Borrowed pattern:** A good lawyer does not control the outcome but still inspires confidence and guides the journey — that is the emotional target for this legal block.
