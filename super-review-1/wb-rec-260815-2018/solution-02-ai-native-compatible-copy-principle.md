# Write all site copy to be AI-native and agent-compatible

Everything Shroffin writes — including the footer Disclaimer they were just editing — should be **AI compatible** / **AI native**.
This is a standing copy rule for the whole site: an agent should be able to listen, understand, and work with the words as if the agent were the user.
They floated “agent is you,” listening, understanding emotions, and even a camera — as what “truly AI native” means for how copy is written.
They said this right after deciding the disclaimer must use Shroffin’s own words, not generic legal boilerplate.

## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

After the disclaimer rewrite talk, they raised a **company writing principle** that applies beyond that one block.

Raw ASR (`audio.srt` 04:07.760–04:13.900): “Even if you don't understand, we have to put our words into it. That's what you said.” — bridging from bespoke Shroffin voice into the AI-native rule.

Then (`audio.srt` 04:19.100–04:31.440): **“We have to always be AI compatible.”** and **“AI native means we are also AI native. Everything we do is AI native.”**

They brainstormed what that means for copy and product voice (`audio.srt` 04:35.860–05:09.660):

- **“It's like agent is you.”**
- **“He is listening.”**
- **“An agent means we understand our emotions.”**
- **“Or we need to have a camera.”**
- **“That is truly AI native.”**
- **“You become AI native.”**
- **“Your life is compatible with an agent.”**

This is **company thinking about how to write**, not a defect on the page. They did **not** click a camera control or point at a missing UI element — they were still idle on the expanded footer Disclaimer (`screenshots/0031.png`–`0037.png`).
