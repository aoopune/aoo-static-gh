## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

They were on Explore banks → Other charges, cycling the **Rate change type** dropdown. After looking at Floating → Fixed (they called that simple), they switched to **Repricing** and said the user has to change the rate themselves. They contrasted that with the repo rate, which “doesn't change automatically.” Then: if you have more CIBIL, you can go to the bank, tell them you have more CIBIL, “change the rate or else I will go to you,” and then the rate changes. They named this: **“This is also intelligence. To save money.”** They added that if you take so much of your CIBIL you will get a loan, but you have to increase it.

A few seconds later, still on this column, they said some people give benchmarks, you **can shift the benchmark**, “Generally people don't do it. But there is an option.” That is the same intelligence job: show the option that saves or changes money, even if few people use it.

This idea can also be the solution for a Rate change column that otherwise only lists fees or “Not published by bank.” Issue `issue-02-rate-change-charge-too-complex-legal-jargon.md` is the defect (too many complexes / legal words). This file is the constructive direction: Shroffin should tell the user the CIBIL-bargaining / benchmark-shift hack.

## What this is for

Explore banks, Other charges tab, **Rate change charge** — especially the **Repricing** type (and later **Benchmark switch**). User-job: after you already have a loan, how do you get the bank to cut the rate and save money.

## Why they said it that way

They treated this as company “intelligence,” not as more legal text. Repo (ASR: “vapor rate”) is not enough because it does not auto-change for the borrower. CIBIL is leverage. They care about saving money, not “tell me the fee.”
