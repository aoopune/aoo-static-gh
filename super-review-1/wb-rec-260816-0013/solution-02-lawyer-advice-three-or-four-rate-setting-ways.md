## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

They opened the **°** note under Rate change charge. The notes define Benchmark switch with MCLR, Base Rate, RLLR, EBLR, EBR and a string of symbols. They said “This needs to be a lot simpler. There is a legal language.” Then the constructive shape: **somewhere it should be like there are 3 to 4 ways banks calculate rates** — they depend on **RBI**, or a **consortium of private banks**, or **their own individual calculation**. Usually RBI is most preferred and most common. If you want these type of facilities, you should go for this type. **“Should feel like an advice coming from a lawyer instead of just technical terms and legal words.”**

That is both an idea and a proposed change for this notes block. Issue `issue-02-rate-change-charge-too-complex-legal-jargon.md` is the defect. This file is the rewrite they asked for.

## What this is for

The Rate change charge **° notes** (and the customer’s job of understanding how a bank sets / changes the rate) on Explore banks Other charges.

A customer cannot use MCLR/LIBOR footnotes. They borrowed the **lawyer** voice (advice, not statute). They also walked who decides MCLR, repo vs MCLR, LIBOR→MIBOR (ASR iBOR), IBA (ASR ABI), and “What is the customer's risk? It is the cost of sleep.”
