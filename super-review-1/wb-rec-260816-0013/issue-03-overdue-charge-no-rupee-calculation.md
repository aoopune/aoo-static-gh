## Exact issue
Still on Other charges, they pointed at overdue percents ("What is 5%?") and said they need a calculation here: how an overdue is made after missing an EMI, how much money, how much charge, how many days until notice, how many days until a collection agency. They opened the ‡ overdue note, then clicked overdue cells (including 2.00% p.a.). They said there is no monitoring value, "Show me the calculation," and "How did you get 2%? You have written the annum. But I put a total amount in the next month. I have not used the money for an annum."

EMI bounce is on the same table but they did not attack bounce as a separate defect in this recording; next recording folds bounce into the same overdue-calculation drawer.
