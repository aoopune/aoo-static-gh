## Exact issue
On Explore banks, Charges tab, they opened government charges for HDFC and Yes Bank. The table showed **₹30,518** for every visible bank. The drawer listed the same four lines for a ₹48,00,000 loan: CERSAI ₹118, MODT stamp duty ₹14,400, NOI filing ₹1,000, NOI registration capped at ₹15,000, total ₹30,518. Notes under the table repeated Maharashtra / India government-charge text. They said this is stamp duty and registration, asked what the NOI line is, and asked how you would know the information is already in the card versus on the screen.

Raw ASR: "It is not a problem. It is time duty." / "What is the yellow or space? Notice of information." / "This is the same. You have the same screen information in the same card." / "How would you know if the information is in the card?" / "And beregistration."

ASR likely meant: stamp duty; NOI (Notice of Intimation); registration. Click + screenshot win over "yellow or space" and "time duty".
