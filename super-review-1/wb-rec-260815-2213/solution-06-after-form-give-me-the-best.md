## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)
They described the user job after this form: feed the customer’s parameters, look at all offers, and say “in minimum efforts, which best offer will fit the customer?” Making that feature is “a one-day job” because it is “completely AI driven,” but “Making a feature is not an option. It is mainly design thinking. Product management. Engineering is after thought. Engineering is not a problem. Product is the problem.” Then they looked at the live form: “This is fine. I think this is fine. Let them do what they want. And here only, we have implemented three things. Then they will not feel that they are getting what they want. Let them see how much they get. In the score of 720 to 780, this will be the best offer. You don't just sit and watch.” CIBIL on screen is 780, inside that band. User story: “that is exactly how I would try to use it. … first fill the form. You tell me. You don't know anything. Give me the best.” That idea can also be the solution for the table that currently makes you sit and watch.
