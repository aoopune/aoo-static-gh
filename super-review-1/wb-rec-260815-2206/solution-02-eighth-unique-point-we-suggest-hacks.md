## Exact solution (or idea that can also be a solution)

They floated an eighth unique point for the beginning of the site. Quoted `audio.srt`: “I was thinking that maybe our 7 unique points at the beginning, there can be an 8th unique point. We don't use market research. We suggest you hacks. Where you can save your maximum money. Basically, there is a profit curve. That you can do so much more with so much effort.” Later they closed the same direction for first impression: “In some form, we should be conveying this from the beginning. That in your minimum efforts, You can save maximum money. Which can fit in the Pareto curve. We are going to tell you all this.” Because of that, the person using the tool “won't feel scared” of taking out intelligence.

This idea can also be the positioning solution for how Shroffin introduces itself. They did not decide copy or a homepage slot. `decidedness: brainstorm` (“maybe”).

## What this is for

Opening unique points / first message of the site (they said “at the beginning”), discussed while idle on Explore banks. Issue-01 is the missing hacks in the tool; this file is the separate idea of saying “we suggest hacks” up front.

## Why they said it that way

If people hear min-effort / max-money first, they will not treat the form as a cold interrogation. Pareto/profit curve names the same small-effort hacks as solution-01, aimed at first impression rather than the calculator itself.
