## First-principles problem
- What must be true: frequency is **two or three times a life**, so people will optimise and get comfortable, not rush a parameter dump. If they believe Shroffin will **tell them** the min-effort / max-money moves, they will not treat Explore banks as a vault to crack.
- Root vs symptom: the inputs card is built like a calculator for someone who already knows the numbers. Fear / probing is the symptom. The root user is the **comfortable, rare, high-stakes** buyer, and the root gap is **silence at the beginning** about the Pareto promise.
- Constraints: job 1 in `05` still exists (today’s offer from exact details) — they are saying that is not **who walks in first**, not that today’s offer should vanish. “In some form”; “from the beginning”; “we are going to tell you all this.”
